# ADR 0013: vehicle as stock location van sales

Status: accepted (2026-09-04).

## Decision

Van sales are supported without a separate app: each vehicle is a stock location; loading posts transfer_out/transfer_in; on-spot orders are fulfilled and invoiced from the vehicle location at delivery; closing counts unsold stock back in before cash settlement. Founder confirmed on 2026-09-04 that crews sell from van stock when a shopkeeper asks. Decision D26.
