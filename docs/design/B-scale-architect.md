# B — Scale-Architect Proposal: Distribution OS

Date: 2026-09-04. Author: principal-architect persona (multi-tenant Indian SMB platforms at Khatabook/Udaan scale). Evidence base: CONTEXT.md plus research reports R01–R10. Optimised for tenant isolation, ledger correctness, offline sync at scale, analytics, cost per tenant, DPDP, and a path to lakhs of users, while acknowledging that one developer builds v1.

---

## 0. Executive summary and the irreversibility register

The product is a distributor-owned, multi-brand system of record. The research is unanimous that nobody sells this to distributors at SMB prices (R01 §1, R04 §4.3, R10 §2.11), that the daily pain is "three billing systems in parallel" (CONTEXT invoices E/F/G), and that a third of a distributor's income depends on schemes and claims that are compiled by hand today (R09 §4, §6). The architecture below is designed so that the ledgers are correct from day one, tenant isolation is a database guarantee, the field apps work with no signal, and the whole thing runs for roughly $55/month at one distributor and about $1,300/month at one hundred.

The most important thing a solo developer can do is spend the first three weeks on the decisions that cannot be changed later. This register is the spine of the proposal.

| Decision                                                                                                                                                             | Reversible later?          | Why it is fixed on day one                                                                                                                                                  |

## 1. Verdict on the five-apps plan

**Keep five products. Ship them as two store binaries plus one web console.** This is a recommended change to the "five apps" decision on packaging, not on scope, and the reasons are store review, permissions, and solo-developer release load (R07 §2.3). The founder gets all five experiences; each is a role module with its own navigation tree, icon, and onboarding, and nothing prevents splitting a module into its own binary later because they share one codebase.

| Product (as decided)   | Ships as                                                                                                                                        | Surface                                                      | Why                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Distributor (owner) | **Owner Web Console** (Vite + React PWA) plus an **Owner module in the Team app**                                                               | Web primary, mobile for approvals, live map, and day numbers | Dashboards, price control, exports, Tally mapping, claim statements and imports are desktop tasks; approvals and "where are my vans" are phone tasks.                 |
| 2. Salesperson         | **Team app** (Expo), role module                                                                                                                | Mobile only, offline-first                                   | Beat, orders, bargain requests, retailer onboarding in-beat, GPS check-in as evidence not gate.                                                                       |
| 3. Manager (warehouse) | **Team app** role module for capture (invoice photos, gate count, pick/pack confirmation, barcode) plus the **Billing Desk** in the Web Console | Mobile for the dock, web for the desk                        | Invoice review on a phone under 60 s is the target (R06 §10); bulk billing, picklists by trip and printing are better on a laptop.                                    |
| 4. Delivery            | **Team app** role module                                                                                                                        | Mobile only, offline-first, trip-scoped GPS                  | Trip stops, delivery states, on-spot orders, cash/UPI, returns, end-of-trip checklist. Also the van-sales "mixed role" (R03 §7, R09 §3).                              |
| 5. Retailer            | **WhatsApp channel first, PWA second, Retailer app (Expo) last**                                                                                | WhatsApp, then web, then native                              | Install friction is the documented adoption killer (R10 §2.14, §3); the native app carries no sensitive permissions so its store review is trivial when it does ship. |

Why two binaries and not one: the Team app carries background location, a location-typed foreground service, and camera; the Retailer app must not. Bundling them would force a Play Console location declaration and disclosure video onto a shopping app (R07 §4.2).

Why one web console and not five web apps: the owner console, the manager's billing desk and the back-office curation queue for the global product master are all the same React application with role-gated routes.

**Deliberately not building** (with the reason, so the founder can push back):

- **A separate van-sales app.** Van sales is answered "yes" as SAP's mixed role: vehicle as a stock location, trip aggregate, on-spot order fulfilled from the vehicle location (R03 §7, R09 §3). No new binary.
- **Route optimisation (VRP).** Owner-defined beats plus a routing API for stop sequence and Google Maps hand-off; revisit at more than five vehicles per tenant (R03 §11).
- **ONDC seller app in the pilot.** Add the order `source` enum now; register the company as NP-MSN at ~20 tenants (R04 §3.5).
- **Outbound IRN/e-invoice generation** until a tenant crosses ₹5 crore and sells to registered retailers; keep the invoice model IRN-ready (R05 §4).
- **Shelf-photo analytics, image recognition, planogram compliance.** Brand-side, model-heavy, low owner value (R03 §14, R10 §2.15).
- **Wallets, lending, BNPL, auto-debit.** Out of scope by founder decision; credit hold and credit-note auto-apply are the borrowed behaviours (R03 §14).
- **No-code configurability.** Opinionated defaults; per-tenant flags only where Tarsun actually diverges.
- **A second datastore (ClickHouse/Timescale/Elasticsearch/Redis)** before a measured need. Postgres does jobs, search, realtime fan-out and analytics until ~100 tenants (R08 §5).
- **Marg DBF or Busy XML readers.** Excel/CSV import profiles instead (R02 §5).
- **Firebase phone auth or Twilio Verify.** 30–60× more expensive than WhatsApp/MSG91 in India (R07 §8).
- **All-day GPS polling of sales reps.** Trip-scoped tracking for delivery only; geo-tag is evidence with owner-visible override, never a billing gate (R01 §5.2–5.3).

## 3. Backend architecture

### 3.1 Language, framework, runtime

TypeScript end to end: **NestJS 12 on the Fastify adapter, Node 24 LTS, Postgres 17 (18 when RDS offers it) as the only stateful system, Drizzle 0.45.x pinned, Zod 4 + oRPC in `backend/libs/contracts`, pg-boss 12 for jobs and the outbox, Better Auth for identity, CASL for abilities, OpenTelemetry** (R08 §1, §4, §5, §7). The reason is not throughput; it is that one person must share GST math, scheme stacking, state machines and validators between a server and five surfaces, and NestJS's module system substitutes for the code-review discipline a team would provide. If the founder dislikes decorators, Hono + oRPC with hand-rolled module folders is the runner-up; the `shared/` packages and the database design are identical either way.

### 3.3 Data model highlights (the irreversible parts, concretely)

**Tenancy and RLS.** Two DB roles: `app_owner` (migrations) and `app_rw` (API and worker; no `BYPASSRLS`, not the owner). Every tenant table:

```sql
ALTER TABLE orders ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY;
ALTER TABLE orders FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY;
CREATE POLICY tenant_isolation ON orders
  USING (tenant_id = (SELECT current_setting('app.tenant_id', true)::uuid))
  WITH CHECK (tenant_id = (SELECT current_setting('app.tenant_id', true)::uuid));
-- costs: additional role predicate
CREATE POLICY cost_visibility ON tenant_product_costs
  USING (tenant_id = (SELECT current_setting('app.tenant_id', true)::uuid)
     AND (SELECT current_setting('app.actor_role', true)) IN ('owner','manager','curator'));
```

Each unit of work: `BEGIN; SELECT set_config('app.tenant_id', $1, true), set_config('app.actor_role', $2, true), set_config('app.actor_id', $3, true); ... COMMIT;` behind transaction-mode pooling (RDS Proxy or PgBouncer). Drizzle also adds `WHERE tenant_id = ?`; a Nest guard refuses any request without a resolved tenant. Global tables (`manufacturers`, `products`, `product_variants`, `hsn_rates`, `product_aliases`, `product_external_codes`, `retailer_identities`) have no `tenant_id`, `SELECT` for all, writes only under `actor_role = 'curator'` or the identity service. The "salesperson never sees margin" test becomes: the `salesperson` role's transaction cannot read `tenant_product_costs` at all, and the PowerSync stream for that role never selects it. A CI test dumps a rep device's SQLite and asserts no cost column exists.

**IDs.** `id uuid DEFAULT uuidv7()` (PG18) or app-generated v7 on PG17; clients generate v7 offline. Human-facing numbers come from `numbering_series(tenant_id, series_code, fy, next_no)` locked with `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` at commit; series per sale type (GL/, GC/, CN/, RCPT/) reset per financial year, 16-character cap for IRN readiness.

**Stock ledger (pieces, lots, same-transaction balance).**

```sql
stock_lots(id, tenant_id, variant_id, batch_no, mrp numeric(10,2), expiry date, mfg date, UNIQUE(tenant_id, variant_id, batch_no, mrp))
stock_ledger(id uuidv7, tenant_id, occurred_at, lot_id, location_id, qty_delta int,
  reason enum[grn, sale, sale_return_saleable, sale_return_damaged, damage, expiry_writeoff,
              transfer_out, transfer_in, van_load, van_unload, adjustment, cycle_count, opening],
  ref_type, ref_id, actor_id, idempotency_key, created_at)
stock_balances(tenant_id, lot_id, location_id, on_hand int CHECK (on_hand >= 0 OR negative_allowed), reserved int, version int, PRIMARY KEY(tenant_id, lot_id, location_id))
reservations(id, tenant_id, order_line_id, lot_id NULL, location_id, qty, state enum[pending, posted, voided])
```

MRP is a lot attribute (R09 §13) because the same SKU sits in stock at two MRPs after a revision and the bill must print the MRP of the lot picked; credit notes carry the original invoice's tax rate. ATP = `on_hand - reserved` per location; the salesperson app and retailer surface read a `sellable_stock` view, never `on_hand`. Vehicles are `locations` of type `vehicle`; check-out is `van_load` at warehouse and `van_unload`... more precisely `transfer_out` warehouse and `transfer_in` vehicle in one transaction with supervisor PIN; check-in is the reverse with counted qty and a `trip_variance` reason on any gap. Partition `stock_ledger` and `trip_points` by month only when a table exceeds RAM (R08 §2.4); the `(tenant_id, occurred_at)` leading key makes that a data move.

**Money ledger.** `accounts` seeded per tenant (AR, Cash, UPI clearing, Sales @5/12/18, Output CGST/SGST/IGST, Discounts, Sales returns, Round off, Scheme receivable per manufacturer, Claims receivable). `journal_entries` balance to the paisa (service check plus deferred constraint trigger). Retailer outstanding is the AR balance for the party; ageing is open invoices minus `allocations`. Receipts from the delivery app allocate FIFO by default (bill-to-bill norm, R09 §8) with a per-tenant override; cash discounts are conditional lines realised only when payment lands inside the window, else reversed by a credit note entry.

```sql

supplier_pack_configs(tenant_id, supplier_id, variant_id, pcs_per_case int, supplier_code text, margin_basis enum[on_mrp, on_ptd])
tenant_product_costs(tenant_id, variant_id, lot_id NULL, purchase_rate numeric, landed_cost numeric, ptd numeric, updated_from_grn_id)
```

Distributor-proposed products go live immediately for that tenant as `proposed` and enter a curation queue in the web console (initially the founder); merges rewrite `product_aliases`, `product_external_codes` and `tenant_products` but never ledger rows (they reference lots, which reference variants; a merge inserts a `merged_into` pointer and the ledger view resolves it).

**Retailer identity across distributors.**

```sql
retailer_identities(id, phone text UNIQUE, gstin text NULL, display_name, consent_notice_version, consent_at, withdrawn_at NULL)   -- global, no tenant_id
retailers(id, tenant_id, identity_id NULL, code text, shop_name, address, lat, lng, beat_id, price_tier_id, credit_limit_amount, credit_limit_bills, credit_limit_days, credit_mode enum[indicate, strict, stop], payment_terms enum[PRE, ON, POST_FULFILLMENT], fssai_no NULL, gst_reg_type enum[regular, composition, unregistered], tally_ledger_name NULL, UNIQUE(tenant_id, code))
retailer_links(identity_id, tenant_id, retailer_id, linked_at, linked_by enum[rep_onboarding, directory_optin, import], status)
```

The retailer's login is the identity; each distributor's `retailers` row is that distributor's private customer record (credit limit, tier, code). The retailer can edit shop details on the identity and the distributor-visible profile; credit and tier columns are never in any retailer-role write contract. A Better Auth organization is the tenant; a retailer identity holds memberships (role `retailer`) in many organizations (R08 §7).

**Pricing and promotions as typed rules, shared TS.**

```ts
type PromotionRule = {
  id; tenantId; schemeId; scope: {brand?|category?|variantIds?|all};
  trigger: {kind:'qty'|'value'|'mix'; min: number; unit:'pcs'|'case'|'inr'; slabs?: Slab[]};
  reward: {kind:'free_qty'|'line_pct'|'order_pct'|'cash_discount_pct'|'net_scheme_amount'; value; freeVariantId?};
  applicability: {tiers?: string[]; retailerIds?: string[]; beatIds?: string[]};
  validFrom; validTo; stackable: boolean; final: boolean; fundingSource:'company'|'distributor'; claimable: boolean;
  gstOnFreeGoods: boolean; pricingDateMode: 'order'|'delivery';
}
```

Evaluation order is fixed: price list tier → retailer override wins → schemes stack unless `final` → cash discount is conditional at settlement (CONTEXT decision, extended with validity dates, slabs, net-scheme conversion, and funding/claimability per R02 §7, R03 §6, R09 §13). The function `priceOrder(order, rules, priceLists, clock)` is pure, deterministic and lives in `backend/libs/domain`; the server recomputes on sync and any difference from the device's total becomes an approval item rather than a silent overwrite.

**Orders.** `sales_orders.source enum[salesperson, retailer_app, retailer_whatsapp, delivery_onspot, brand_dms_import, ondc_partner, manual]`; three state machines as decided: fulfilment (draft → confirmed → authorised → picked → packed → dispatched → delivered/partial/cancelled), delivery (per stop: pending → started → arrived → delivered/partial/failed with reason), invoice-payment (issued → partially_paid → paid → written_off; credit notes as separate documents). States are named so that `on_status` for ONDC B2B 2.0.2 is a lookup table, not a refactor (R04 §6).

### 3.4 API style

- **oRPC procedures with Zod 4 input/output** in `backend/libs/contracts`, served by NestJS over HTTP; TanStack Query bindings for web and mobile; the same router emitted as OpenAPI for the Tally Connector, importers and future brand or ONDC adapters (R08 §4). Not GraphQL (no need for client-driven shapes; ledger writes want explicit commands), not bare REST (loses end-to-end types for one developer), not tRPC (oRPC gives OpenAPI for free).
- Every mutating procedure declares `idempotencyKey: uuid`; a Nest interceptor implements Stripe semantics (`idempotency_keys(tenant_id, key, request_hash, status, body)`, replay on retry, error on param mismatch, prune after 24 h).
- **`POST /sync/upload`** is the one non-oRPC endpoint: PowerSync's upload queue posts local transactions; the handler maps table ops to domain commands, returns 2xx always for business rejections (writing a `sync_errors` row the device syncs back) and 5xx only for transient faults (R07 §3.2). This endpoint is a wire protocol; version it from day one (`X-Sync-Protocol: 1`).
- Webhooks in: WhatsApp Cloud API, GSP callbacks, Tally Connector polling (outbound pull, no inbound ports on the customer LAN).

### 3.5 Background jobs

pg-boss 12 in the same Postgres (R08 §5.1): outbox rows are pg-boss jobs enqueued inside the business transaction. Queues: `outbox.*` (read-model updates, notifications), `docint.extract`, `docint.escalate`, `notify.whatsapp`, `notify.sms`, `notify.push`, `reporting.daily_rollup` (cron 00:30 IST), `inventory.rederive_balances` (nightly, alerts on drift), `receivables.ageing_snapshot` (nightly), `claims.window_reminders`, `gst.ewb_expected_deliveries` (every 6 h when credentials exist), `gst.taxpayer_token_refresh` (every 5 h while a session exists), `integrations.tally_export`, `integrations.import_batch` (rate-limited), `dpdp.erasure`, `dpdp.gps_retention` (drops raw `trip_points` older than 90 days, keeps trip summaries). Move only notification fan-out to BullMQ on Valkey if pg-boss ever exceeds ~10% of primary CPU.

### 3.6 Realtime

SSE from the API for the owner dashboard, approvals queue and order status (fan-out across instances via `LISTEN/NOTIFY` carrying ids, not documents). WebSocket only for the live delivery map (GPS points arrive through PowerSync anyway; the map subscribes to a per-tenant channel fed by the `trip_points` insert trigger). Retailers get WhatsApp, not push (CONTEXT).

### 3.7 Auth and RBAC

Better Auth self-hosted: `phoneNumber` plugin (OTP via Send hook → WhatsApp authentication template ₹0.115, MSG91 SMS fallback), `organization` plugin (organization = tenant; roles owner, manager, salesperson, delivery, retailer, curator), `jwt` plugin exposing JWKS so PowerSync verifies short-lived JWTs carrying `tenant_id`, `user_id`, `role`, `beat_ids` (R07 §3.2, R08 §7). Sessions are DB-backed and revocable from the owner console (rep quits → device dies). Three authorization layers: (1) org role → transaction settings; (2) CASL abilities in `backend/libs/domain` (`cannot('read','Product',['purchasePrice'])` for reps) run identically in apps and API; (3) RLS and the cost-table split as the database's last word. OTP rate limits per phone and per IP; monthly SMS budget alarm.

## 5. Offline-first sync design

### 5.1 Engine

**PowerSync** over Postgres logical replication, op-sqlite on device, PowerSync Cloud Free for the pilot moving to Pro ($49/month) around ten distributors, with the self-hosted Open Edition on ECS Mumbai as the fallback if PowerSync Cloud cannot offer an India region (verify before pilot; this is a data-residency question, not a functional one). Reasons: ElectricSQL has no write path and its cloud is winding down; WatermelonDB is dormant; Replicache is in maintenance; Zero rejects offline writes; RxDB charges for RN SQLite and leaves the Postgres side to you (R07 §3). PowerSync's read side (buckets, checkpoints, partial sync that re-evaluates on reassignment, attachment queue) is the highest-risk code a solo developer could otherwise write.

### 5.4 Idempotency and ordering

Each device holds a monotonically increasing op id per local transaction; the upload includes `(device_id, op_id)`, which the server stores in `idempotency_keys` alongside the domain-level `idempotencyKey` uuid. Retries after a 5xx replay the stored response; a 2xx is final. Business rejections write `sync_errors(tenant_id, user_id, device_id, op_id, table, row_id, code, message_hi, message_en, created_at)`, which is itself synced to the device that caused it and surfaced in an "Needs attention" tray. The queue never wedges because the server never returns 4xx to the uploader (R07 §12.2).

### 5.5 Attachments and GPS

Invoice pages, POD photos, damage photos and gate-count photos go through PowerSync's attachment queue to S3 via pre-signed PUT; metadata rows sync normally. Trip-scoped GPS uses `expo-location` + `expo-task-manager` with an Android location foreground service ("Trip in progress — location shared with Tarsun Enterprises"), iOS background indicator, `Balanced` accuracy, 50 m / 30 s deferral, `AutomotiveNavigation`, a `location_consents` row captured with the prominent-disclosure dialog, and Transistorsoft as the $399 escape hatch for OEM battery killers (R07 §4.3).

## 11. Recommended changes to CONTEXT decisions (flagged, with reasons)

1. **Five products, two binaries plus a web console** instead of five separate apps (store review scope, permissions, release load; R07 §2.3). Scope unchanged.
2. **Van sales: yes**, as the SAP mixed role with the vehicle as a stock location and a Trip aggregate; no separate app (R03 §7, R09 §3).
3. **Retailer surface sequencing: WhatsApp → PWA → native app** (adoption evidence, R10 §5.1). The retailer product is still built; its native binary ships last.
4. **Add Claim and Scheme aggregates** with funding source, claimability and stamping of scheme ids on every invoice line (a third of distributor income; R09 §13, R10 §2.4).
5. **MRP as an attribute of the stock lot (batch + MRP)**, dated HSN rates, and supplier-specific pack configs (R09 §13).
6. **Pricing engine ships to the device as a pure module**; server recompute on sync opens an approval on mismatch rather than overwriting silently (R03 §4).
7. **Order `source` enum and ONDC-shaped vocabularies now**; no ONDC build until ~20 tenants (R04 §6).
8. **"Zero manual entry" becomes "zero manual typing except the gate box count"**; keep a Claude/Gemini disagreement log from day one (R06 §9.3).
9. **Tally-XML-compatible export and a Windows Tally Connector** are first-class integration scope; brand-DMS secondary invoices are ingested through document intelligence (R01 §7.9, R02 §7).
10. **Own-VPA UPI QR with UTR capture**, aggregator QR opt-in only (R10 §5.4).
11. **Add Marathi** for the Thane pilot alongside Hindi and English (R10 §5.2).
12. **Geo-tag is evidence with owner-visible override, never a billing gate**; delivery GPS is trip-scoped, not all-day (R01 §7.2–7.3).
13. **Publish pricing and a visible feature-request channel** from the pilot onward (R01 §7.8, R02 §7).

None of these contradict the ledger, aggregate, tenancy, identity, GST, document-intelligence or trade-credit decisions already made; they extend them.
