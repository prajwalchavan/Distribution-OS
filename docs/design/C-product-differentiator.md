# C — Distribution OS: product-differentiator architecture proposal

Author: product-led founder/architect lens. Date: 2026-09-04. Evidence base: CONTEXT.md, R01–R10. Nothing below contradicts a CONTEXT decision unless it appears in section 11 ("Recommended changes") with reasons.

---

## 0. The bet, in one page

**What distributors pay for.** A Kalyan distributor already spends Rs 8–26k one-time plus Rs 2–5k/yr on Marg, Rs 8–24k/yr on Tally, and is forced to run Too Yumm's brand DMS for free (R02, CONTEXT invoice E). Nobody sells him a _multi-brand system of record he owns_; the enterprise vendors concede the gap and solve it by extracting from his Tally (R01 §1). He will pay a subscription when three things are visibly true within two weeks of parallel run: (1) inbound stock enters by photo with no typing (nobody else has this, R01 §4), (2) his pending-bills file is on the phone with ageing, UPI QR and WhatsApp reminders (R09 §8, R10 §3), (3) his CA gets Tally XML and GST-ready registers without asking (R02 §6, R01 §5.7). Everything else is retention, not conversion.

**What makes salespeople and delivery crews use it daily.** Six years of top-voted complaints on Bizom, FieldAssist and Botree are the same four: blocking sync, geo-fence false negatives that stop billing, all-day battery drain, logout on phone calls (R01 §5). So the field app must be local-first with no "sync now" button, geo-tag as evidence not gate, trip-scoped GPS only for delivery, and a session that survives a phone call. The rep's order screen opens on "last order / suggested" with case-or-pieces toggle and pending-undelivered qty inline (R03 §3). The delivery crew's screen is the stop list, one-tap delivered/partial with reason codes, a UPI QR, and an end-of-trip checklist that reconciles cash (R03 §9, R10 §2.9).

**What makes retailers use it.** Retailers do not install apps for one distributor; they order on WhatsApp in Hinglish ("bhai 200 amul 1L, deliver kal", R10 §2.1) and rate ordering apps 4.1–4.5 stars while one-starring the physical execution behind them (R04 §2). So the retailer surface is WhatsApp first (every invoice, receipt and POD photo arrives there automatically), a PWA second, a store app last; and the distributor's OS guarantees the execution that every brand app fails at: reservation-aware stock, delivery state only from the stop with proof, doorstep return with instant credit note, retailer-visible outstanding.

**Architecture that serves those bets.** TypeScript end to end; one NestJS modular monolith on Postgres in Mumbai; a pure pricing/GST/state-machine domain package that ships to phones; PowerSync for offline-first partial sync; Claude vision plus deterministic Indian-invoice validators for document intelligence; three frontend codebases (Team app, Retailer app, Owner console) for five products; Tally-XML and CSV exports as first-class integration surfaces.

## 1. Verdict on the five-apps plan

**Keep five products. Ship them as three codebases and two store binaries.** This is a recommended refinement, not a contradiction: the founder's five apps are five role experiences, and each still exists as its own navigation root, icon-level entry, onboarding and permission set.

| Product (CONTEXT)      | Where it lives                                                                          | Form factor                                                                                                                          | Why                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Distributor (owner) | `apps/team` (owner role) + `apps/console` (web)                                         | Mobile for live dashboard, approvals, live map, coverage; web for price lists, scheme setup, Tally mapping, imports, reports, claims | Dense tables and CSV/XML work are miserable in React Native; the owner also sits at a PC with the CA (R02 §6.2).                                                                          |
| 2. Salesperson         | `apps/team` (rep role)                                                                  | Mobile, offline-first                                                                                                                | Beats, order taking, bargain requests, my retailers. Needs camera (shop photo) and foreground location only during check-in.                                                              |
| 3. Manager (warehouse) | `apps/team` (manager role) + `apps/console` for billing desk                            | Mobile for invoice scanning, GRN review, packing confirmation; web for picklist printing and bulk billing                            | The scan-and-review flow is phone-first (R06 §10); billing 200 orders is a keyboard job.                                                                                                  |
| 4. Delivery            | `apps/team` (delivery role)                                                             | Mobile, offline-first, background location while a trip is running                                                                   | The only role that needs `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION` and a location foreground service (R07 §4).                                                                                         |
| 5. Retailer            | `apps/retailer` (Expo web build = PWA first, store binaries later) plus WhatsApp intake | WhatsApp → PWA → native                                                                                                              | Install friction is the documented adoption killer (R04 §5.3, R10 §2.14). Expo Router builds the same code to web and native, so "PWA first, app later" is a build target, not a rewrite. |

**Why two store binaries and not five.** The Team app is the only binary that carries background location, foreground-service type, and camera-for-documents permissions, which are the permissions that trigger Play Console declaration forms and demo videos (R07 §4.2). Keeping those out of the Retailer app keeps its review trivial and its permission list short. Five binaries would mean five EAS build pipelines, five store listings, five quarterly SDK upgrades (R07 §12 pitfall 10) for one developer. A role-gated app also makes the "van sales / mixed role" answer free: a delivery crew that takes on-spot orders uses the rep's order screen against the vehicle location (R03 §7).

**Mobile vs web split, explicitly.**

- Mobile only: order taking, visits, delivery stops, ePOD, collections, trip GPS, invoice photo capture, GRN review, packing confirmation, owner live map, owner approvals.
- Web only (`apps/console`): price lists and scheme master, Tally mapping and export, CSV/Marg/Tally importers, GST registers, claim statements, global catalog curation (your back-office role), tenant settings, user/device management, retailer merge tools.
- Both: owner dashboard (mobile summary, web detail), retailer ledger, order queue.

**Sequencing by dependency for a solo developer.** The founder wants everything at once; the honest answer is that every screen reads from two ledgers (stock, money) and one aggregate (Sales Order), so those are built first and the five apps are populated in the order their data appears. The full week plan is in section 9; the dependency chain is:

```
tenancy + identity + contracts  →  catalog (global) + tenant-catalog
  →  retailers + beats  →  pricing engine (pure) + schemes  →  Sales Order aggregate
  →  stock ledger + lots + locations  →  docint GRN (populates stock)
  →  rep order screen (populates orders)  →  billing + receivables (populates money)
  →  warehouse pick/pack  →  delivery trips + ePOD + collections + GPS
  →  owner dashboard + approvals + console  →  retailer WhatsApp intake + PWA
  →  claims, incentives, Tally connector, EWB, suggested order, credit banding
```

## 3. Backend

### 3.1 Language, framework, runtime

TypeScript everywhere. **NestJS 12 on the Fastify adapter, Node 24 LTS, Postgres 17** (R08 §1). The reason is not throughput; it is that one person must share Zod contracts, the pricing engine, GST math and three state machines between the server and three apps, and that Nest's module boundaries substitute for a team's code review. Drizzle 0.45.x (pinned until v1 final) with `drizzle-kit` SQL migrations and RLS policies as code; raw `sql` tags for ledger statements. Zod 4 in `shared/contracts`. **oRPC** for typed procedures with TanStack Query bindings and a generated OpenAPI surface for integrations. pg-boss 12 for jobs and the transactional outbox (no Redis at launch). Better Auth (self-hosted) with `phoneNumber`, `organization` and `jwt` plugins. CASL abilities in `shared/domain`. OpenTelemetry via `@nestjs/observe` to Grafana Cloud, errors to Sentry.

Runner-up if decorators grate: Hono + oRPC with hand-rolled module folders (R08 §1.2). Do not use Bun for the ledger.

### 3.3 Data model highlights

**Tenancy.** Shared schema, `tenant_id` on every tenant table, `ENABLE` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`, app role `app_rw` that is neither owner nor `BYPASSRLS`, transaction-local `set_config('app.tenant_id', …, true)` and `set_config('app.actor_role', …, true)` set by a Nest interceptor at the start of every unit of work (pooler-safe). Policies read `(SELECT current_setting(...))` and reference only current-row columns. ORM adds `WHERE tenant_id = ?` as defence in depth (R08 §2).

**IDs.** UUIDv7 as `text` primary key on every synced table, generated on the client (PowerSync requires `id text`; offline creation requires client IDs, R07 §3.2). Human-facing numbers (invoice `GL/1686`, GRN no, trip no) are server-assigned from a per-tenant per-series sequence at commit, never on the client.

**Global master with tenant overlay.** `manufacturers`, `brands`, `products`, `product_variants` (net qty, unit, default case size, MRP history), `hsn_codes` (dated rates; GST 2.0 of 22 Sept 2025 makes rate a dated attribute), `product_external_codes` (manufacturer + code), `product_aliases` (supplier GSTIN + normalised text) have no `tenant_id`; writes only via a curator role; distributor proposals promoted after review. `tenant_products` is the overlay (listed, sell UoM rules, case-size override, supplier pack conversion). **`tenant_product_costs`** is a separate table whose RLS predicate also requires `actor_role IN ('owner','manager')`, so the Vyapar failure is a database guarantee, and the rep sync stream selects only sell-side columns (R07 §3.2, R08 §2.3).

**Retailer identity across distributors.** `retailer_identities` (global: phone E.164, optional GSTIN, optional FSSAI, auth user id) and `retailers` (tenant-scoped: code, shop name, address, lat/lng from device at first visit, beat, credit terms, tier, band, tally ledger name, brand-DMS ids like `FO_GFIL_…`). A shop linked to many distributors is many `retailers` rows pointing at one identity; the retailer PWA shows one card per distributor. Retailer can edit shop details on its identity/row; credit limit, tier and band columns are excluded from the retailer-role `WITH CHECK` policy.

**Stock ledger.** `stock_ledger(id, tenant_id, occurred_at, variant_id, lot_id, location_id, qty_delta pcs, reason enum [grn, sale, sale_return_saleable, sale_return_damaged, damage, expiry, transfer_out, transfer_in, van_load, van_unload, adjustment, count_variance], ref_type, ref_id, actor_id, idempotency_key)`; `stock_balance(tenant_id, variant_id, lot_id, location_id, on_hand, reserved, version)` maintained in the same transaction with `CHECK (on_hand >= 0)` unless the tenant allows negative; reservations follow pending/post/void. **Lots** = `(variant, batch_no, mrp, expiry)` — MRP is a lot attribute, not a SKU attribute (R09 §13). Quantities in pieces; cases are display via case size; loose pieces are just a balance that is not a multiple of case size.

**Money ledger.** `journal_entries` / `journal_lines` (paisa-exact, `SUM(debit)=SUM(credit)` enforced by deferred trigger), `accounts` (AR, Cash, UPI clearing per VPA, Sales @rate, Output CGST/SGST/IGST, Discounts, Sales returns, Round off, Claims receivable per manufacturer), `receipts` (mode cash/upi/cheque, UTR, collected_by, trip_id) and `allocations(receipt_id, invoice_id, amount)`. Outstanding = AR balance per party; ageing over open invoices minus allocations. Credit notes reverse at the original invoice's tax rate.

**Sales Order aggregate.** `sales_orders(source enum [salesperson, retailer_app, whatsapp, delivery_onspot, brand_dms, ondc_partner, import], retailer_id, rep_id, beat_id, pricing_date_mode, payment_term enum [PRE_FULFILLMENT, ON_FULFILLMENT, POST_FULFILLMENT], fulfilled_from_location_id, fulfilment_state, requested_delivery_date)` and `sales_order_lines(variant_id, qty_pcs, qty_input_uom, unit_price, applied_rules jsonb [{rule_id, kind, amount|free_qty}], free_qty, line_taxable, gst_rate, reserved_lot allocations after pick)`. Three independent state machines live in `shared/domain`: fulfilment (draft → submitted → approved → reserved → picked → packed → dispatched → delivered/partial → closed/cancelled), delivery (per stop: pending → arrived → delivered/partial/failed[reason]), invoice-payment (issued → part-paid → paid → written-off; credit notes attach).

**Pricing/schemes.** `price_lists(tier, valid_from/to)`, `price_list_items`, `retailer_price_overrides(valid_from/to)`, `scheme_rules(kind enum [free_qty, line_pct, order_pct_on_payment, qty_slab_rate, net_scheme_amount, inbound_gst_benefit], scope, trigger, reward, valid_from/to, tier/retailer applicability, stackable, is_final, funding_source [company, distributor], claimable, brand_id, claim_window_days)`. Every invoice line stores `applied_rules` so claims can be reconstructed and the bill prints Free / Scheme % / Disc Rs / Cash Dis % columns exactly as invoices E and F do.

**Trip aggregate.** `vehicles` (also a `locations` row), `trips(vehicle_id, crew[2], planned_stops, checked_out_at, checked_out_by, checked_in_at, expected_cash, declared_cash, expenses, variance, settlement_status)`, `trip_stops(order_ids, sequence, state, reason_code, pod{photo, otp?, signature?, lat/lng, ts})`, `trip_points(trip_id, device_ts, lat, lng, accuracy, speed)` (partition candidate).

**Claims.** `claims(manufacturer_id, type enum [scheme, damage, expiry, display, rate_difference, transport], period, window_ends_at, lines[{invoice_line|return|grn_discrepancy ref, amount}], evidence[], status [accrued, ready, submitted, settled, rejected], settlement_mode)`.

### 3.4 API style

- **oRPC procedures** (Zod in/out) under `shared/contracts`, served by Nest; the apps consume them with TanStack Query for online-only calls (dashboard, reports, docint status). The same router is exposed as OpenAPI JSON for the Tally Connector, importers and any future brand or ONDC adapter. No GraphQL (no need for client-shaped queries; PowerSync handles local reads). REST-shaped webhooks for Meta WhatsApp, GSP callbacks and the connector.
- **PowerSync upload endpoint** `POST /sync/upload` is one procedure that takes the client's local transaction batch (with per-client op ids), runs domain validation inside one Postgres transaction with tenant context, and returns 2xx always for business rejections (writing a `sync_errors` row the device syncs back), 5xx only on transient faults (R07 §3.2 rule 5).
- **Idempotency** interceptor on every mutating procedure: `Idempotency-Key` header (UUID), `idempotency_keys(tenant_id, key, request_hash, status, body)` checked and written in the same transaction; parameter mismatch → 409.
- **Realtime**: SSE `@Sse()` for owner dashboard, approvals queue and order status, fanned out through `LISTEN/NOTIFY` (ids only); one WebSocket namespace for the live delivery map.

### 3.5 Background jobs

### 3.6 Auth and RBAC

## 5. Offline-first sync design (Team app and Retailer app)

**Engine: PowerSync** over Postgres logical replication, op-sqlite on device (R07 §3). ElectricSQL is winding down, WatermelonDB is dormant, Zero rejects offline writes. Start on PowerSync Cloud Free (2 GB/mo, 50 clients) for the pilot; plan the self-hosted Open Edition on ECS in Mumbai (or PowerSync Cloud Pro) by ~10 tenants for residency and cost control (verify PowerSync Cloud region availability in India before the pilot; see risks).

**Partial sync scope (Sync Streams keyed on JWT claims).**

| Stream             | Subscribed by     | Query shape                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Notes                                                                                                    |
| ------------------ | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `reference` (auto) | all               | global `products`, `product_variants`, `brands`, `hsn_codes`, `uom`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | no tenant filter; no cost columns exist here                                                             |
| `tenant_sell_side` | all staff         | `tenant_products_sell_view`, `price_lists`, `price_list_items`, `scheme_rules`, `locations`, `vehicles`, tenant settings WHERE `tenant_id = auth.parameter('tenant_id')`                                                                                                                                                                                 | the view excludes cost/margin by construction                                                            |
| `rep_scope`        | salesperson       | `retailers` WHERE `beat_id IN (SELECT beat_id FROM rep_beat_assignments WHERE rep_id = auth.user_id())`, `retailer_price_overrides` for those retailers, `sales_orders`+lines WHERE `rep_id = auth.user_id() AND created_at > now()-90d`, `visits`, `bargain_requests`, `retailer_outstanding_summary` (pre-aggregated: balance, oldest bill days, band) | reassignment re-evaluates the parameter query and the device re-syncs                                    |
| `delivery_scope`   | delivery          | `trips` WHERE crew contains user AND date ≥ today-2, `trip_stops`, orders/invoices for those stops, `receipts` for those trips, `trip_points` for own trips                                                                                                                                                                                              | retailer phone column hidden after stop is delivered via a per-stop view                                 |
| `manager_scope`    | manager           | open `sales_orders`, `picklists`, `stock_balance` for warehouse + vehicles, `docint_jobs` and review state, `grn` last 30d                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |                                                                                                          |
| `owner_scope`      | owner             | `daily_tenant_stats`, approvals queue, `trip_summaries`, coverage rollups, `tenant_product_costs`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | the only stream that carries costs; dashboards are pre-aggregated because stream queries cannot GROUP BY |
| `retailer_scope`   | retailer identity | per linked distributor: listed catalog with prices from the pricing engine inputs, own orders, own invoices, own outstanding, deals                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | via `retailer_memberships` parameter query                                                               |

Never sync: `tenant_product_costs` to non-owner roles, other reps' retailers, full order history, raw GPS of other trips. CI includes a "rep device dump" test that asserts no cost column reaches the rep bucket (R07 §12 pitfall 4).

**Write path and conflict rules.**

- All writes are local-first into SQLite, queued in PowerSync's FIFO upload queue, posted to `/sync/upload` with per-client op ids; server is authoritative and recomputes.
- **Orders**: client creates `draft` and `submitted` orders and lines with client UUIDs and the client-computed price preview (from the pure engine). Server re-runs the engine at upload with server-side price lists and credit rules; if totals differ it stores the server result and writes a `sync_errors`/`order_notices` row ("price updated: scheme X expired") that the rep sees; if credit hold or MOV fails, the order lands in the approvals queue rather than being rejected. After `approved`, line edits are server-only actions (delivery partials, returns), so the only concurrent edit surface is draft/submitted; per-field LWW there is acceptable because one rep owns one draft. Retailer-app and rep both editing the same draft is prevented by ownership (`created_by`), not merged.
- **Collections / receipts**: append-only. A receipt is never edited; a mistake is a reversing receipt. Allocation to invoices is computed server-side (bill-to-bill oldest first unless the crew tagged specific invoices). Duplicate upload of the same receipt id is idempotent.
- **Delivery states**: transitions validated by the state machine on the server; an out-of-order arrival (offline device sends `delivered` after the manager cancelled the stop) yields a `sync_errors` row and an approval item, never a silent overwrite.
- **GPS**: `trip_points` append-only, primary key `(trip_id, device_ts)` dedupes replays; points outside a running trip are dropped.
- **Docint review edits**: single reviewer per job (job locked to a device), so LWW is safe.
- **Reference and price data**: server-only writes; devices never write those tables.
- Checkpoints: the device does not advance to a new checkpoint while its own mutations are unacknowledged, so a rep never sees half-applied state (R07 §3.2 rule 6).

**Idempotency end to end.** Client UUIDv7 ids for rows; PowerSync op id per transaction; `Idempotency-Key` on every online oRPC mutation; server `idempotency_keys` table; ledger rows carry `idempotency_key` with a unique index per tenant; human numbers assigned at commit.

**No "sync now" button.** A small status dot (synced / pending n / offline) and a "last synced" line; sync runs in the background on connectivity change and app foreground; expo-background-task flushes the queue every 15 minutes when the app is closed.

**Attachments.** Photos (invoice pages, ePOD, shop photos, damage) go through PowerSync's attachment queue straight to S3 via pre-signed URLs, metadata rows sync normally; thumbnails cached locally.

**Zero-training UX rules (enforced in the design system).**

1. Every screen opens on the most likely action: rep → "Aaj ka beat" list with reorder buttons; delivery → next stop; manager → GRN queue and today's orders; retailer → "Reorder last order".
2. Numeric input is a keypad, never a text field; qty stepper defaults to the retailer's last quantity; case/pcs toggle shows both ("2 case = 180 pcs").
3. State is always colour + icon + word in the user's language; no state is only a colour.
4. No modal that blocks work for sync, GPS or permissions; a persistent banner instead. Geo-fence outside → amber flag on the visit, never a block.
5. Everything the retailer sees (price, scheme, free qty, outstanding) is the same object the invoice prints from.
6. Owner numbers are rupee outcomes first (collected today, cash in transit, at-risk stock, claims due), features second.
7. Hindi and English are toggleable from every screen header; product names stay in brand form.

**Definition of production-grade.**

- Tests: `shared/domain` at ≥90% line coverage with golden fixtures from invoices A–G (pricing, GST, rounding, state machines); integration tests on real Postgres for every module including RLS negative tests ("rep role SELECT on tenant_product_costs returns 0 rows", "retailer cannot update credit_limit"); sync contract tests (upload endpoint returns 2xx + sync_errors on business rejection); rep-device-dump test; Maestro e2e for four critical mobile flows; docint eval score tracked in CI against the fixture set.
- Monitoring: the alert list in §7, an on-call phone (the founder) with a paging rule for API down, backup failure and ledger drift; weekly cost report.
- Backups: PITR + Object-Locked nightly dump + monthly restore drill logged in `infra/runbooks`.
- Security: forced RLS, secrets in Secrets Manager, OTP rate limits and spend alarm, pre-signed URLs with short TTL, audit log on price/credit/role changes, dependency scanning, signed native builds, DPDP notices and retention job (GPS raw traces 90 days), 72-hour breach runbook, penetration checklist before the second paying tenant.
- Operability: expand/contract migrations, blue/green with rollback, EAS Update rollback, runbooks for the nine failure modes in section 10.

## 10. Top 10 risks and mitigations

| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Solo-dev scope: "all five at once" stalls everything**                                                                                               | Dependency-ordered milestones (§9) where each milestone is demoable to Tarsun; role-gated single binary; console for dense screens; no VRP/ML/ONDC/fintech in the pilot.                                                                                                                |
| 2   | **Sync engine risk** (PowerSync region/residency, licence, or a future wind-down like Electric)                                                        | Server-authoritative design keeps all business logic in Postgres + Nest; PowerSync is replaceable at the `frontend/packages/sync` boundary; self-host Open Edition (FSL → Apache) planned by ~10 tenants; verify India region before pilot.                                             |

| 5   | **Store rejection or OEM battery killers break delivery GPS**                                                                                          | Trip-scoped tracking with FGS notification, disclosure dialog, Play declaration form and video prepared in week 22; Transistorsoft fallback budgeted; GPS points stored locally and synced later so gaps do not lose data.                                                              |

## 11. Recommended changes to CONTEXT decisions (flagged, with reasons)

1. **Five products, three codebases, two store binaries** (Team app role-gated; Retailer app; Owner console web). Reason: store-review scope, permission declarations, release load (R07 §2.3); dense screens belong on web.
2. **Retailer surface order: WhatsApp → PWA → native**, using Expo web output so it is one codebase. Reason: install friction is the documented adoption killer (R04 §5.3, R10 §5.1).
3. **Van sales: yes, as the SAP "mixed role"** — vehicle as stock location, trip aggregate, on-spot orders from the vehicle; no separate app (R03 §7, R09 §3).
4. **Pricing engine is a pure module that ships to devices**, server recomputes on sync; validity dates on every price level; `pricing_date` mode per tenant (R03 §4, §13).
5. **Add Claim and Scheme-funding aggregates**; stamp scheme ids on every invoice line (R09 §13, R10 §5.3).
6. **MRP and expiry are lot attributes** (batch + MRP), HSN rates are dated (R09 §13).
7. **Pack hierarchy per SKU with supplier-specific conversions**; docint parses case-size suffixes for confirmation (R09 §13).
8. **LR + GRN damage annotation is mandatory before receipt closes**; "zero manual entry" becomes "zero manual typing except the blind gate count" (R06 §9.3, R09 §13).
9. **Tally-XML-compatible export/endpoint and brand-DMS invoice ingestion are first-class integration scope** (R01 §7.9, R02 §7).
10. **Order source enum and ONDC-shaped vocabulary now** (payment term PRE/ON/POST_FULFILLMENT, min_qty/case_size, serviceable pincodes, FSSAI/GST on tenant), no ONDC build in pilot (R04 §6).
11. **FSSAI number on every bill template**; own-VPA UPI QR with UTR capture, aggregator opt-in (R09 §13, R10 §5.4).
12. **Geo-tag is evidence, never a billing gate; no blocking sync step; trip-scoped GPS only for delivery** (R01 §7.2–7.3).
13. **Add Marathi soon after pilot** for Thane; keep Hindi + English first (R10 §5.2).
14. **Publish pricing** with a floor around Rs 2,000–2,500/month base + Rs 150–300 per field seat, unlimited retailers, WhatsApp quota; free tier capped by bills/month (R10 §4, §7 cost table).
15. **Log extraction confidence and every human correction from day one** so a later opt-in "accept all" or per-retailer autopilot is possible without changing "never auto-commit" (R03 §13.3, R05 §7.6).
