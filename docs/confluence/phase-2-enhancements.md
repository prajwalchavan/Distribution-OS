# Why this page exists

Every requirement, change and enhancement that is **not in version 1** is written down here, with the reason it is not in version 1 and what would start it. If an idea is not in version 1 and not permanently ruled out, it has a row below.

This page is a register, not a roadmap. A row here has a reason and a trigger, not a date.

---

# The v1 boundary

A row belongs on this page only if it is **not** in version 1. These are all in version 1 and are therefore _not_ deferred:

- **All AI features:** WhatsApp free-text and voice order capture parsed into a draft order (always confirmed by a person), demand forecasting and reorder suggestions, and route sequencing that the driver may override.
- **The shopkeeper's side of the app:** the shop places orders and pays online.
- **The platform console:** a separate app and service for Distribution OS staff — distributor onboarding, plans and subscription state, time-boxed owner-approved support access.
- **The generic importer, Tally XML export, brand-DMS import, claims, incentives, notifications, reporting and document intake.**
- **One codebase for web, Android and iOS.** The six business roles use one app — one website and one Android app; the iOS app is built from the same code and is not released yet.
- **A Welcome screen and a landing screen:** before sign-in, the Distribution OS mark, the app's own name and one **Sign in** button, shown once per device and not on every launch; after sign-in, the distributor's (or the shop's) logo and name, the person's name and which app this is. Built once in `@dos/ui` and used by the app and the console.
- **Role election at sign-in, downward only, with extra roles set by the owner or a manager:** an owner may act as any staff role, a manager as the field roles, everyone else as their own role plus the extra roles set on their membership. The token carries the **elected** role, so the services, the permission matrix and row-level security apply to it unchanged and the person remains the actor on every audit row; an owner token is never let into a field app.
- **One app — "Distribution OS" — that becomes the right app after sign-in:** the six business roles are one Expo project, served as one website at `www.distributionos.in` and one Android app. The platform console is a separate app.
- **Offline for the field roles:** `@dos/offline`, the product's own delta-sync client, is in the **sales, delivery and warehouse** screens, and sign-out on a shared device leaves nothing of the previous person or distributor behind.

# Reading the register by horizon

The register is one list on purpose — a single place to look. The rows fall into three horizons by their **trigger**, not by their importance:

1. **Next after version 1** — P2-33 (observability, uptime and crash reporting), P2-34 (the availability work beyond one server) and P2-44 (the iOS app).
2. **Triggered by use at the first distributor** — P2-01, P2-02, P2-05, P2-10, P2-12, P2-18, P2-19, P2-23, P2-31, P2-35, P2-36, P2-37. Each one starts when real use produces the evidence named in its trigger; several will never start, which is the point of writing the trigger down.
3. **Triggered by the second and third customer, or by scale** — P2-03, P2-04, P2-06, P2-09, P2-11, P2-13 to P2-17, P2-20 to P2-22, P2-24 to P2-30, P2-32, P2-38, P2-39, P2-40, P2-41.

The two rows most likely to be needed first are **P2-09 multi-branch** (the first multi-branch prospect makes it a sales blocker, and it is a tenancy change, not a screen) and **P2-01 OTP** (the moment a real user cannot reset a password). Both are cheap to start and expensive to retrofit late.

# How a row leaves this page

A row leaves this register when its work becomes part of the product; this page never holds an item that is already in version 1.

A row is **not** a commitment. The trigger column is the test: until the trigger fires, building the row costs more than it returns.

# Ruled out permanently (not Phase 2)

These are not deferred — the product does not do them, and none of them waits for a trigger.

- **Fintech of any kind**: payments aggregation, wallet, lending, BNPL. Revenue is the distributor's subscription; collection is the distributor's own UPI VPA and UTR.
- **Per-tenant custom roles and no-code workflow building.** Settings, feature flags, numbering series, credit modes, schemes, tolerances and POD policy are configurable; roles, state machines, approval kinds and the permission matrix are fixed and tested. **Role election does not change this:** it grants a person an existing fixed role, downward from their own, and creates no new role.
- **Redis, Kafka, microservices or a second operational datastore.**
- **Shelf-photo and planogram analytics; live chat for bargaining; all-day GPS of sales representatives; geo-fence gates that block billing or check-in.**
- **Binary readers for Marg DBF or Busy XML** — the generic importer takes Excel and CSV.
- **OCR-then-LLM pipelines, specialist parsers and on-device vision models** — document intake is LLM vision with validators and a human review.
- **Consumer e-commerce, HR and manufacturing ERP**, and replacing the accountant's Tally — the journal and the Tally export complement it.
- **Per-device licensing and opaque pricing.**
