# Why this page exists

**Decided 2026-09-05 (founder):** every requirement, change and enhancement that has been **deferred** is written down here, with the reason it was deferred and what would make us start it. Nothing is allowed to be "dropped in conversation". If an idea is not in v1 and not permanently ruled out, it has a row below.

This page is the **parking lot with a process**, not a roadmap. A row here has a reason and a trigger, not a date. Dates are set only when a row is promoted into a build chain.

**Source of truth.** `docs/22-source-of-truth.md` in the repository is the master; its §8 dated decisions and §9 non-negotiables win over anything on this page. The deferral evidence comes from `docs/24-confluence-alignment.md` (the audit of every statement in this space against the build), the screen-and-gap inventory `docs/23-app-screens-and-api-gaps.md`, the scope document `docs/03-scope-and-must-not-build.md`, and the design system `docs/design/UX-00-design-system.md`. Where this page and the repository disagree, **the repository wins** and this page is corrected.

**Build context.** As at 2026-09-05 13:45 IST: **14 backend modules verified, 1,442 automated tests, 1,004 endpoint calls exercised, 0 broken** (Build Status & Roadmap mirrors `docs/18-build-log.md`). No app screen is built yet. Everything below sits **after** that chain finishes.

# The v1 boundary

A row belongs on this page only if it is **not** in the v1 chain. To keep the boundary honest, these were all **moved into v1** and are therefore _not_ deferred:

- **All AI features. Decided 2026-09-05:** WhatsApp free-text and voice order capture parsed into a draft order (always human-confirmed), demand forecasting and reorder suggestions, and route sequencing that the driver may override. This overrides the earlier "post-pilot after a 100-utterance benchmark" deferral and the "no route optimisation" line in `docs/03`.
- **The retailer app. Decided 2026-09-05:** the shop places orders and pays online in v1. The v1.0 space marked this persona "(Future)".
- **The platform console. Decided 2026-09-05:** a seventh app and service — distributor onboarding, plans and subscription state, time-boxed owner-approved support access.
- **The generic importer, Tally XML export, brand-DMS import, claims, incentives, notifications, reporting and document intake** — all queued modules in the v1 chain.
- **Every app on web, Android and iOS. Decided 2026-09-04**, replacing the "Web / Mobile per persona" matrix.

The gaps listed in `docs/23` §8.4–§8.10 (delivery, docint, integrations, claims, notifications, reporting, incentives) are **v1 chain work, not Phase 2**, and are deliberately absent below.

# Reading the register by horizon

The register is one list on purpose — a single place to look. For planning, the rows fall into three horizons by their **trigger**, not by their importance:

1. **Already committed, sequenced after the backend** — P2-07 (offline for sales and delivery, before the pilot) and P2-34 (deployment, backups and the restore drill, at go-live). These are v1 work with a "later" position in the queue, not open questions.
2. **Triggered by the pilot itself** — P2-01, P2-02, P2-05, P2-10, P2-12, P2-18, P2-19, P2-23, P2-31, P2-35, P2-36, P2-37. Each one starts when the pilot produces the evidence named in its trigger; several will never start, which is the point of writing the trigger down.
3. **Triggered by the second and third customer, or by scale** — P2-03, P2-04, P2-06, P2-08, P2-09, P2-11, P2-13 to P2-17, P2-20 to P2-22, P2-24 to P2-30, P2-32, P2-33, P2-38.

The two rows most likely to be needed first are **P2-09 multi-branch** (the first multi-branch prospect makes it a sales blocker, and it is a tenancy change, not a screen) and **P2-01 OTP** (the moment a real user cannot reset a password). Both are cheap to start and expensive to retrofit late, so they are the two worth designing for while v1 is still being built.

# How a row leaves this page

1. The founder gives a decision; it is written into `docs/22-source-of-truth.md` §8 **in the same turn**, with the date.
2. The row is removed from this register and appears as a module plan and a build-log entry.
3. This page never holds an item that is already in a v1 chain — if it does, it is a bug in this page.

A row is **not** a commitment. The trigger column is the test: until the trigger fires, building the row costs more than it returns.

# Ruled out permanently (not Phase 2)

These are not deferred — they are decided against, and a proposal to build one needs a new founder decision, not a promotion from this page.

- **Fintech of any kind**: payments aggregation, wallet, lending, BNPL. Revenue is the distributor's subscription; collection is the distributor's own UPI VPA and UTR.
- **Per-tenant custom roles and no-code workflow building.** Settings, feature flags, numbering series, credit modes, schemes, tolerances and POD policy are configurable; roles, state machines, approval kinds and the permission matrix are fixed and tested.
- **Redis, Kafka, microservices or a second operational datastore.**
- **Shelf-photo and planogram analytics; live chat for bargaining; all-day GPS of sales representatives; geo-fence gates that block billing or check-in.**
- **Binary readers for Marg DBF or Busy XML** — the generic importer takes Excel and CSV.
- **OCR-then-LLM pipelines, specialist parsers and on-device vision models** — document intake is LLM vision with validators and a human review.
- **Consumer e-commerce, HR and manufacturing ERP**, and replacing the accountant's Tally — the journal and the Tally export complement it.
- **Per-device licensing and opaque pricing.**
