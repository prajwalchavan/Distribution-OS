# Why this page exists

**Decided 2026-09-05 (founder):** every requirement, change and enhancement that has been **deferred** is written down here, with the reason it was deferred and what would make us start it. Nothing is allowed to be "dropped in conversation". If an idea is not in v1 and not permanently ruled out, it has a row below.

This page is the **parking lot with a process**, not a roadmap. A row here has a reason and a trigger, not a date. Dates are set only when a row is promoted into a build chain.

**Source of truth.** `docs/22-source-of-truth.md` in the repository is the master; its §8 dated decisions and §9 non-negotiables win over anything on this page. The deferral evidence comes from `docs/24-confluence-alignment.md` (the audit of every statement in this space against the build), the screen-and-gap inventory `docs/23-app-screens-and-api-gaps.md`, the scope document `docs/03-scope-and-must-not-build.md`, the design system `docs/design/UX-00-design-system.md`, the sign-in and one-app design `docs/29-sign-in-roles-and-one-store-app.md`, and the programme plan `QA/10-DAY-PLAN.md` with the live quality record `QA/STATE.md`. **Build context — 21 September 2026.** Both halves of the product are built. **Backend complete** (2026-09-06): 23 modules, eight services, 139 tables, ~2,400 automated tests. **Frontend complete** (2026-09-07): all seven apps — owner, manager (with the accountant on it), sales, warehouse, delivery, retailer and the admin console — each one Expo codebase serving website, Android and iOS, each passed by an independent gate that walked every screen at desk and phone widths. The "no app screen is built yet" of version 2.0 of this page is obsolete.

# The v1 boundary

A row belongs on this page only if it is **not** in the v1 chain. To keep the boundary honest, these were all **moved into v1** and are therefore _not_ deferred:

- **All AI features. Decided 2026-09-05:** WhatsApp free-text and voice order capture parsed into a draft order (always human-confirmed), demand forecasting and reorder suggestions, and route sequencing that the driver may override. This overrides the earlier "post-pilot after a 100-utterance benchmark" deferral and the "no route optimisation" line in `docs/03`.
- **The retailer app. Decided 2026-09-05:** the shop places orders and pays online in v1. The v1.0 space marked this persona "(Future)".
- **The platform console. Decided 2026-09-05:** a seventh app and service — distributor onboarding, plans and subscription state, time-boxed owner-approved support access.
- **The generic importer, Tally XML export, brand-DMS import, claims, incentives, notifications, reporting and document intake** — all queued modules in the v1 chain.
- **Every app on web, Android and iOS. Decided 2026-09-04**, replacing the "Web / Mobile per persona" matrix.
- **A Welcome screen and a landing screen in every app. Decided 2026-09-21:** before sign-in, the Distribution OS mark, this app's own name and one **Sign in** button, shown once per device and not on every launch; after sign-in, the distributor's (or the shop's) logo and name, the person's name and which app this is. Built once in `@dos/ui` and used by all seven. Design: `docs/29` §1.
- **Role election at sign-in, downward only, with owner- or manager-set extra roles. Decided 2026-09-21:** an owner may act as any staff role, a manager as the field roles, everyone else as their own role plus the extra roles set on their membership. The token carries the **elected** role, so the services, the permission matrix and row-level security are untouched and the person remains the actor on every audit row; an owner token is never let into a field app. Design: `docs/29` §2.
- **One store app — "Distribution OS" — that becomes the right app after sign-in. Decided 2026-09-21:** the six business apps merge into one Expo project, which is one website, one Android app and one iOS app. The seven per-role web apps are **retired** at the merge rather than kept beside it (founder, the same evening: two front doors would be two things to prove, for ever). The platform console stays separate. Design: `docs/29` §3.
- **Offline for the field apps — built, so P2-07 and P2-08 leave this register.** `@dos/offline`, our own delta-sync client (it replaced PowerSync on 2026-09-05), is in the **sales, delivery and warehouse** apps, and the shared-device sign-out rule behind it was closed on measured evidence across Android, iOS and the browser on 2026-09-20.

Two of those three decisions — role election and the one app — were designed for **after** launch. The founder moved them in front of it on 2026-09-21 — _"make sure of role election and one store app for both APPS and website before go live"_ — so that the seven-day business simulation runs on the app that actually ships. Stated plainly, that costs about two more days before go-live.

The gaps listed in `docs/23` §8.4–§8.10 (delivery, docint, integrations, claims, notifications, reporting, incentives) were **v1 chain work, not Phase 2** — every one of those modules is now built and gated — and are deliberately absent below.

# Reading the register by horizon

The register is one list on purpose — a single place to look. For planning, the rows fall into three horizons by their **trigger**, not by their importance:

1. **Dated — they fire at or just after go-live on 27 September 2026** — P2-33 (observability, uptime and crash reporting), P2-34 (what is left of the availability work now that deployment, backups and the restore drill have been promoted) and P2-44 (the complete iOS and Android pass, once the product is otherwise right). These are queue positions with a date on them, not open questions.
2. **Triggered by the pilot itself** — P2-01, P2-02, P2-05, P2-10, P2-12, P2-18, P2-19, P2-23, P2-31, P2-35, P2-36, P2-37, P2-43. Each one starts when the pilot produces the evidence named in its trigger; several will never start, which is the point of writing the trigger down.
3. **Triggered by the second and third customer, or by scale** — P2-03, P2-04, P2-06, P2-09, P2-11, P2-13 to P2-17, P2-20 to P2-22, P2-24 to P2-30, P2-32, P2-38, P2-39, P2-40, P2-41, P2-42.

The two rows most likely to be needed first are still **P2-09 multi-branch** (the first multi-branch prospect makes it a sales blocker, and it is a tenancy change, not a screen) and **P2-01 OTP** (the moment a real user cannot reset a password). Both are cheap to start and expensive to retrofit late. The 21 September cuts add the next two: **P2-42** (a larger automated suite) and **P2-40** (accessibility) both name customer #2 or a public product as their return event, and that is the commercial step immediately after this pilot.

# How a row leaves this page

1. The founder gives a decision; it is written into `docs/22-source-of-truth.md` §8 **in the same turn**, with the date.
2. The row is removed from this register and appears as a module plan and a build-log entry.
3. This page never holds an item that is already in a v1 chain — if it does, it is a bug in this page.

A row is **not** a commitment. The trigger column is the test: until the trigger fires, building the row costs more than it returns.

# Ruled out permanently (not Phase 2)

These are not deferred — they are decided against, and a proposal to build one needs a new founder decision, not a promotion from this page.

- **Fintech of any kind**: payments aggregation, wallet, lending, BNPL. Revenue is the distributor's subscription; collection is the distributor's own UPI VPA and UTR.
- **Per-tenant custom roles and no-code workflow building.** Settings, feature flags, numbering series, credit modes, schemes, tolerances and POD policy are configurable; roles, state machines, approval kinds and the permission matrix are fixed and tested. **Role election (2026-09-21) does not change this:** it grants a person an existing fixed role, downward from their own, and creates no new role.
- **Redis, Kafka, microservices or a second operational datastore.**
- **Shelf-photo and planogram analytics; live chat for bargaining; all-day GPS of sales representatives; geo-fence gates that block billing or check-in.**
- **Binary readers for Marg DBF or Busy XML** — the generic importer takes Excel and CSV.
- **OCR-then-LLM pipelines, specialist parsers and on-device vision models** — document intake is LLM vision with validators and a human review.
- **Consumer e-commerce, HR and manufacturing ERP**, and replacing the accountant's Tally — the journal and the Tally export complement it.
- **Per-device licensing and opaque pricing.**
