# Purpose

This document identifies operational pain points observed during distributor research and maps them to their business impact, root causes, and the solution **as actually built or scheduled** in Distribution OS. It serves as the primary justification for product features and helps prioritize development based on customer value rather than assumptions.

**New in version 2.0.** Every "Proposed Solution" from version 1.0 is replaced by **Solution in Distribution OS** — the named module, procedure or table that answers the pain point — plus an honest **Status**. Nothing from version 1.0 has been deleted; where a dated founder decision changed the answer, the sentence says so and carries its date. The product name is **Distribution OS** (decided 2026-09-05); the six role apps are "Distribution OS - Owner", "- Manager", "- Sales", "- Warehouse", "- Delivery", "- Retailer", plus a seventh internal admin console.

**Status vocabulary.** **BUILT** = every named element exists as a verified backend procedure or table · **BUILT, GAP NAMED** = core built, a named part is missing and stated · **PLANNED (v1)** = not built, a named module in the chain covers it before the pilot · **NOT IN V1** = deliberately out of scope, reason stated.

As at 2026-09-05 13:45 IST: **14 backend modules verified, 1,442 automated tests, 1,004 endpoint calls exercised, 0 broken** (Build Status & Roadmap mirrors `docs/18-build-log.md`). **No app screen exists yet** — backend first is a deliberate sequencing decision (2026-09-04). Statuses below describe the backend, not the user interface.

**Analysis framework.** Each pain point is analyzed as: Current Situation · Root Cause · Business Impact · Severity · Frequency · Affected Personas · **Solution in Distribution OS** (replaces "Proposed Solution", version 2.0) · **Status** · Related Modules.

# Pain Point 1 – Manual Order Entry

**Current situation.** Orders arrive through phone calls and WhatsApp messages. A data entry operator manually enters every order into billing software to generate invoices. **Root cause.** No digital order capture · multiple communication channels · no structured order format.

**Business impact.** High manual effort · slow processing · typing errors · duplicate work · delayed dispatch. **Severity:** Critical · **Frequency:** Daily

**Affected personas.** Sales Representative, Owner, Manager. The **Data Entry Operator persona is designed away** rather than supported (decided 2026-09-04): orders are captured by the rep or the shop and the invoice is generated by the system at pack time, so no re-keying step remains for an operator to own.

**Solution in Distribution OS.**

- **Mobile order capture** — `orders.create`, `orders.setLines`, `orders.submit`, `orders.repeatLast`, priced on the device by the pricing engine before submit.
- **Every order carries its channel** — `source` = `salesperson` | `retailer_app` | `van_sale` | `phone` | `whatsapp`, so the channel mix is measurable from day one.
- **Web order entry** for genuine phone orders — the manager keys them in the Manager app as `source = phone`.
- **The invoice is never typed** — it is derived from the confirmed pack (`warehouse.packs.confirm` → billing issues the GST invoice). Zero re-keying is structural, not a matter of discipline.
- **WhatsApp free text and voice capture are in v1, not "future"** (decided 2026-09-05, overriding the version 1.0 "(future)" label): text or speech is parsed against the shop's own SKU history into a **draft** order that a human always confirms.

**Status.** Order capture **BUILT**. WhatsApp and voice intake **PLANNED (v1)** — module 12 `ai`, with inbound message capture in notifications. **Related modules:** orders, pricing, ai, notifications.

# Pain Point 2 – WhatsApp-Based Order Management

**Current situation.** Sales representatives send retailer orders as free-text messages in WhatsApp groups. **Root cause.** No centralized order management system.

**Business impact.** Missed orders · duplicate orders · difficult searching · no tracking · no order history. **Severity:** Critical · **Frequency:** Daily

**Solution in Distribution OS.** Structured order creation in the Sales app (`orders.create` / `setLines` / `submit`), searchable history through `orders.list` filtered by shop, salesperson, state and date range, and `pricing.bargains.request` for a price the rep cannot give alone. **Refinement, decided 2026-09-05:** the goal is no longer to _replace_ WhatsApp but to _absorb_ it. Shops and reps keep the channel they already use; a free-text message becomes a draft order through the `ai` intake parser, always human-confirmed before it counts. Removing WhatsApp was never realistic; making it structured is.

**Status.** Structured capture and history **BUILT**. WhatsApp absorption **PLANNED (v1)**. **Related modules:** orders, notifications, ai.

# Pain Point 3 – Invoice Becomes the Entire Workflow

**Current situation.** A single printed invoice is used as bill, picking list, delivery note, payment tracker and delivery confirmation.

**Business impact.** If the invoice is lost or damaged, operational information is lost. There is no real-time status visibility. **Severity:** Critical · **Frequency:** Daily

**Solution in Distribution OS.** Each job the paper invoice used to do is its own record: `picklists` and `pick_lines` (picking), `pack_confirmations` (packing), `load_sheets` and `delivery_challans` (loading and dispatch), `trip_stops` and `deliveries` with `pod_evidence` (delivery confirmation), `receipts` and `allocations` (payment tracking). The invoice keeps exactly one job — the legal financial document. It is **issued at pack** by the warehouse (decided 2026-09-04), it is **immutable once issued**, corrections are credit or debit notes, and a cancelled invoice keeps its number. Enforced by database triggers, not by convention.

**Status.** **BUILT.** **Related modules:** warehouse, billing, delivery, receivables.

# Pain Point 4 – No Order Tracking

**Current situation.** Once an invoice is printed, management has no visibility into order progress: has the warehouse picked it, is it loaded, is it on the vehicle, is it delivered? **Severity:** Critical · **Frequency:** Daily

**Solution in Distribution OS.** Order lifecycle tracking with defined states. **The version 1.0 example ladder is replaced by the states actually coded** — a state machine is the only thing permitted to move a state column:

`draft` → `submitted` → `confirmed` → `picking` → `packed` → `dispatched` → `delivered` | `partially_delivered` → `closed`

| Version 1.0 example step | In the product (the rest map one to one)                                                   |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Allocated                | Not a state — stock reservation is a side effect of `confirmed`                            |
| Loaded                   | Not an order state — `warehouse.loadSheets.confirm`, which moves the order to `dispatched` |
| Out for Delivery         | Not an order state — the trip is `active`, the stop is `started` / `arrived`               |

Two further lifecycles run alongside the order: **trip** (`planned` → `loading` → `active` → `closing` → `settled` | `settled_with_variance`) with **stop** (`pending` → `started` → `arrived` → `delivered` | `partial` | `failed`), and **invoice** (`draft` → `issued` → `partially_paid` → `paid`; "overdue" is computed, never stored). Every transition is written to `order_state_transitions` with the actor and device id, so "who moved it and when" is answerable for any order.

**Status.** **BUILT.** **Related modules:** orders, warehouse, delivery, billing.

# Pain Point 5 – Shop Location Problems

**Current situation.** Drivers receive a photograph or vague description of the shop location.

**Business impact.** Delayed deliveries · increased calls · wrong locations · reduced productivity. **Severity:** High · **Frequency:** Daily

**Solution in Distribution OS.** Coordinates on the shop record (`retailers.upsert` with lat/lng), a geo-tag captured at every sales check-in (`visits`) and every delivery arrival (`trip_stops.arrivedLat/Lng`), and a hand-off to the phone's own maps app from `delivery.stops.next`. **Correction to version 1.0:** there is **no "verified location" flag**. Verification is accumulated evidence — the rep's check-in geo-tag and the driver's arrival geo-tag against the stored coordinate. The geo-tag is **evidence, never a block**: a rep with weak GPS can still check in and take the order. The shop is shown an ETA, never a live coordinate of the vehicle.

**Status.** **BUILT.** **Related modules:** retailers, delivery.

# Pain Point 6 – Payment Tracking

**Current situation.** Drivers write payment status on paper invoices; outstanding balances are updated manually after returning to the office.

**Business impact.** Delayed financial visibility · reconciliation effort · lost payment records. **Severity:** Critical · **Frequency:** Daily

**Solution in Distribution OS.** `delivery.collections.record` writes, in one transaction: a receipt row, a **balanced double-entry journal** (balance enforced by a database trigger at commit), and allocation against the oldest open bill first unless the shop tags a specific invoice. Cash, UPI with UTR and cheque are supported, with `receipts.deposit` and `receipts.bounce` covering the cheque life cycle including bank charges and the bill reopening. **Who may take money, decided 2026-09-04:** the **delivery crew** at the door, the **shop itself** paying online in the Retailer app, or the **desk** (owner, manager, accountant) for money received at the office. The **salesperson never records a receipt** — the sales service has no receipt endpoint and the permission matrix never grants one. This is a non-negotiable, tested per endpoint per role. Offline is first-class: collections queue through `sync.upload`, which never answers a 4xx — rejections come back as durable, visible errors rather than lost payments.

**Status.** **BUILT.** Online payment by the shop exists as `receivables.payments.initiate`; the gateway callback is wired at deployment. **Related modules:** delivery, receivables, billing, platform (sync).

# Pain Point 7 – Inventory Visibility

**Current situation.** Inventory is updated only after billing or manual entry; warehouse staff rely on experience rather than accurate system data.

**Business impact.** Stock shortages · overselling · incorrect commitments. **Severity:** High · **Frequency:** Daily

**Solution in Distribution OS.** An **append-only `stock_ledger`**: a row is written at goods receipt (`procurement.grns.post`), reservation (`orders.confirm`), pick and pack (`warehouse.packs.confirm`), load-out (godown → vehicle), return (a credit note restocks), and every adjustment, transfer or cycle count (`inventory.*`). Balances are derived, never edited; every mutation carries an idempotency key, so a retried phone request cannot double-count stock. Available-to-promise comes from the `sellable_stock` view, so the rep's device shows what can actually be sold — on hand minus already reserved — rather than a book figure.

**Status.** **BUILT.** **Related modules:** inventory, procurement, warehouse, orders.

# Pain Point 8 – Warehouse Operations

**Current situation.** Picking depends on staff familiarity with product locations. No rack or bin management exists.

**Business impact.** Slow picking · picking errors · staff dependency. **Severity:** Medium · **Frequency:** Daily

**Solution in Distribution OS.** Consolidated pick lists by SKU across the wave, **FEFO lot suggestion** (first expiry, first out) so the picker is told which batch to take, per-line short-pack reasons, and a pack confirmation that becomes the invoice. Stock locations exist as `warehouse`, `vehicle`, `damaged`, `in_transit` and `customer`. **Honest scope call.** There is **no rack, bin or zone model and no barcode scanning** in v1. The only QR the product reads is the supplier's e-invoice QR on an inbound bill. A godown of this size gains more from FEFO and short-pack discipline than from a bin hierarchy nobody maintains; bins are revisited when a customer's godown actually needs them.

**Status.** Pick lists, FEFO and short reasons **BUILT**. Racks, bins, zones and barcode scanning **NOT IN V1** (previously "Phase 2"). **Related modules:** warehouse, inventory.

# Pain Point 9 – Delivery Management

**Current situation.** Drivers receive printed invoices without optimized routes or live tracking.

**Business impact.** Longer delivery times · difficult monitoring · no ETA visibility. **Severity:** High · **Frequency:** Daily

**Solution in Distribution OS.** Trips with sequenced stops, a manager-approved load-out (the **manager gives the approval from the Manager app**, decided 2026-09-05 — it is not a PIN typed on the warehouse phone), live vehicle positions (`delivery.vehicles.positions` fed by `/gps/points`), proof of delivery by photo, signature or OTP, partial and failed outcomes with reasons, returns as credit notes, van sales from vehicle stock, and end-of-day settlement with a cash variance the owner must clear. **GPS is trip-scoped:** points are captured during a trip only, under a recorded consent (`location_consents`), retained 90 days. The shop sees an ETA, never a coordinate. **Route optimisation is in v1** (decided 2026-09-05, overriding the earlier "must not build" position): stop sequencing by distance and time window, with the driver always free to override the suggested order.

**Status.** Trips, stops, GPS, POD, collections, van sales and settlement **BUILT** (verified 2026-09-05). Route optimisation **PLANNED (v1)** — module 12 `ai`. **Related modules:** delivery, ai, billing, receivables.

# Pain Point 10 – Limited Business Insights

**Current situation.** Management receives end-of-day reports but lacks operational visibility throughout the day.

**Business impact.** Slow decisions · reactive management · missed opportunities. **Severity:** High · **Frequency:** Daily

**Solution in Distribution OS.** Every operational register is already live — open orders, outstanding by ageing bucket, active trips, the billing queue — so today's answer is a list, not a report run at night. On top of that, the reporting module adds the **owner dashboard** (a rolling summary refreshed every fifteen minutes), drill-down registers (rep productivity, delivery performance, fill rate, stock value, scheme spend), and alerts through notifications (dues reminders, deliveries due today). **Owner graphs are a requirement, not a nice-to-have** (decided 2026-09-04): the Owner app must show growth and performance as charts wherever possible, which is why reporting serves chart-ready series — month grain, year on year, grouped by beat — rather than only tables.

**Status.** Live registers **BUILT**. Dashboard, registers, chart series and alerts **PLANNED (v1)** — modules 9 `reporting` and 8 `notifications`. **Related modules:** reporting, notifications.

# Pain Point 11 – Customer Credit Visibility

**Current situation.** Sales representatives often do not know a retailer's outstanding balance or credit limit when taking new orders.

**Business impact.** Excessive credit exposure · delayed collections · credit policy violations. **Severity:** High · **Frequency:** Daily

**Solution in Distribution OS.** The rep sees the shop's outstanding, ageing and ledger (`receivables.outstanding.get`, `receivables.ledger.get`, scoped to their own beats), and the order screen runs `receivables.creditCheck` **before** submit: inside the limits the order auto-confirms, over the limit it becomes an approval request the owner or manager clears. Credit terms are set by the back office only (`retailers.setCredit` — limit, open-bill count, days, mode). **Decided 2026-09-05:** the accountant is the money desk and may record receipts, deposits, cheque bounces and write-offs and may read and export everything, but may **not** set credit limits, prices, schemes, approvals or settings. **The rep sees the dues and never collects them** — see Pain Point 6. Visibility and collection are deliberately separated.

**Status.** **BUILT.** **Related modules:** receivables, retailers, orders, pricing.

# Pain Point 12 – Fragmented Systems

**Current situation.** Different tasks are handled across billing software, WhatsApp, phone calls, Excel and paper.

**Business impact.** Duplicate data · inconsistent information · difficult auditing. **Severity:** Critical · **Frequency:** Daily

**Solution in Distribution OS.** One database, one API contract, one set of master data: a globally curated catalogue of manufacturers, products and variants with a per-distributor overlay; a global shop identity with per-distributor links, so a shop buying from three distributors is one identity with three relationships and one login. Backend modules never read each other's tables — they talk through published services and an outbox — which is what keeps the audit trail whole. **Legacy systems coexist rather than being switched off** (decided 2026-08, reaffirmed 2026-09-04): a **generic importer** (upload → preview → map columns → save the profile → dry run → commit) takes data from TradeEzee, Marg, Busy, Tally, FieldAssist or plain Excel; Tally export keeps the accountant's existing workflow; a sale already billed in a brand's DMS is imported and linked, **never re-invoiced**.

**Status.** Unified platform and shared master data **BUILT**. Generic importer, FieldAssist import and Tally export **PLANNED (v1)** — module 6 `integrations`. **Related modules:** platform (tenancy), catalog, retailers, integrations.

# Product Design Implications

Each implication below is now a property of the built system rather than an intention:

- **Mobile-first workflows for field users.** Six role apps, each web + Android + iOS (decided 2026-09-04). Sales and Delivery work offline before the pilot; an offline upload never returns an error that loses work.
- **Workflow-based processing instead of paper-driven operations.** State columns move only through coded state machines; the invoice is one record among many, not the workflow itself.
- **Real-time status tracking across all operational stages,** with every transition attributed to an actor and a device.
- **Shared master data to eliminate duplicate entry** — one catalogue, one shop identity across distributors.
- **Configurable where it should be, fixed where it must be** (clarified 2026-09-05). Configurable per distributor: branding and logo, numbering series, credit modes and limits, schemes, settlement tolerance, proof-of-delivery policy, feature flags. Deliberately **not** configurable: state machines, the seven roles, approval kinds and the per-endpoint permission matrix — a distributor cannot invent a workflow state or grant a salesperson the ability to collect cash.
- **White-labelled by default** (decided 2026-09-04). The distributor's own name and logo appear inside the app and on every printed document; Distribution OS branding appears only on the sign-in screen.
- **AI-ready is now AI-shipping** (decided 2026-09-05), under the "AI drafts, a human commits" guardrail above.
- **Cost is invisible by construction.** Purchase cost, landed cost and margin are unreadable by the salesperson, warehouse, delivery and retailer roles — enforced by database row-level security with tests, not by hiding a field in the interface.

# Success Criteria

Distribution OS should demonstrably reduce or eliminate these pain points. Each criterion is measurable from data the system already records; the Success Metrics page names the table or procedure behind each.

- **Minimizing manual data entry** — orders captured digitally as a share of all orders (`sales_orders.source`); supplier bill lines committed without an edit (module `docint`, once built).
- **Increasing operational visibility** — time from order submitted to invoice issued (`order_state_transitions`); share of the day's orders visible in a live register rather than an end-of-day report.
- **Improving inventory accuracy** — counted versus expected at cycle count (`cycle_count_lines`); short-pack rate.
- **Reducing delivery delays** — on-time rate from stop ETA versus arrival; failed-delivery rate and reason mix.
- **Accelerating payment reconciliation** — average days to allocate a receipt against a bill; cash variance at trip settlement.
- **Enabling informed, data-driven decision-making** — owner dashboard adoption and the ageing profile trend, once module 9 `reporting` lands.
