# Purpose

This page is the **register of every decision that binds the Distribution OS product** — what was decided, when, why, and where in the build it is actually enforced. It is the page the Home page refers to as the Founder Decisions Register.

It exists so that no decision is re-litigated by accident. If a proposal contradicts a row below, the row wins until the founder changes it and a new dated row is added here.

**Source of truth.** `docs/22-source-of-truth.md` §8 in the repository is the master register; this page mirrors it. Engineering decisions come from `docs/15-decisions-log.md` and the architecture decision records in `docs/adr/`. Where this page and the repository disagree, **the repository wins** and this page is corrected.

As at 2026-09-21: **the product is built**. The backend is complete — 23 modules across eight services, 139 tables, 34 migrations — and **all seven apps are built and gated green** (7 September), each a single codebase serving website, Android and iOS. Quality assurance has since been run against the built product in two batches: batch 1's 4 P0 and 30 P1 order-to-cash defects are fixed, and **batch 2 is merged at 153 of 158 findings**. What remains is a **seven-day run to go-live — live by Saturday 27 September** if the books balance on the Thursday: the cross-role chain, role election, the merge of six apps into one, a seven-day business simulation on that one app, repair, then Android basics, the security slice and hosting. Build status mirrors `docs/18-build-log.md` and `QA/STATE.md`; the plan is `QA/10-DAY-PLAN.md`. A decision being recorded here means it is **decided**, not that it is shipped — the "Where enforced" column says which.

# How to read this log

- **Newest first.** Within a date, the most consequential decision is listed first.
- **Decision** states the rule in the form a builder can obey.
- **Why** is the reason it was chosen over the alternative, not a restatement of the rule.
- **Where enforced** names the file, table, procedure or document that makes it true. `Superseded` in this column means a later row replaced it; the replacing row is named.
- Founder decisions and engineering decisions are in **one table**, because in a solo build they constrain each other.
- Rows carrying a `DOS-nnn` code come from the quality-assurance programme: a defect or a question that walking the built product raised, and that the founder or the architect then settled. The finding registers are `QA/findings/`.

# Reversals worth knowing

These rows overturned something a stakeholder may still remember. Items 1–10 carry the audit code from `docs/24-confluence-alignment.md` §5.

1. **The salesperson does not collect money** (C1). The August persona listed collections as a sales KPI; the sales service has no receipt endpoint — and since 13 September the offline upload door refuses it too.
2. **The invoice is issued at pack, after picking, by the warehouse** (C2). There is no data-entry-operator role and no pre-picking invoice.
3. **Six role apps, each web and mobile** (C4), not a web-only matrix per persona and not two store binaries. Reversed again on 21 September — see item 11.
4. **The accountant does not approve credit** (C5). Since 13 September the accountant also does not write orders and does not add stock.
5. **The retailer app is current scope, not "future"** (C6): the shop places orders and pays online.
6. **Permissions are one fixed, tested matrix** (C7). There are no per-tenant custom roles and no configurable order statuses.
7. **No branch entity in v1** (C8) — one tenant is one distributorship.
8. **Route sequencing and AI order capture are back in scope** (C9, C16), reversing the earlier "must not build" line, by the 2026-09-05 AI decision.
9. **A platform console exists** (C14), reversing the earlier "no platform console" position, by the 2026-09-05 decision.
10. **The repository is the source of truth, not this space** (C12, C13).
11. **ONE app in the store, not seven** (2026-09-21). The six business apps merge into a single install that becomes the right app after sign-in, and **the seven per-role web apps are retired at the merge** rather than kept beside it. The platform console stays separate. The seven backend services are unchanged — the merge is a front-door decision, never a security one.
12. **A person is no longer locked to one role at sign-in** (2026-09-21). Role election grants a role downward from the membership — an owner may act as a driver for a morning — which reverses the assumption that one membership means one app. What is *not* reversed: an owner token is never issued to a field app, and the person, not the elected role, stays the actor on every audit row.
13. **Hosting is Oracle Cloud Always Free, not AWS Lightsail** (2026-09-21, on verified terms). Free *managed* Postgres turned out to be impossible for this schema — creating the worker role that bypasses row-level security needs a superuser, which Neon, Supabase and RDS all withhold — so Postgres is **self-hosted** on an always-free VM in Mumbai, the apps go on Cloudflare Pages, and the domain is `distributionos.in`. This supersedes the "Lightsail Mumbai as the starting compute" clause of the 2026-09-05 deployment row; the rest of that row (dev stays local, self-managed Postgres until ~10 paying tenants, all-in-one process mode, our own delta sync, Android first) is unchanged.

# How this log is kept

1. A decision is written into `docs/22-source-of-truth.md` §8 **in the same turn it is made**, with its date. This page is updated from that file.
2. A decision is only recorded once it names **where it is enforced** — a file, a table, a procedure or a document. A decision with no enforcement point is an intention, and belongs on the Goals page instead.
3. A decision is never edited in place. It is superseded by a new dated row, and the old row stays with `Superseded` in its last column.
4. Architecture decisions that would be expensive to reverse also get an ADR in `docs/adr/`, listed in the irreversibility register `docs/adr/0000-irreversibility-register.md`.
5. The build status behind these decisions lives in `docs/18-build-log.md` and `QA/STATE.md` and changes daily. This page changes only when the product changes.
