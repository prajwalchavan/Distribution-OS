# Positioning and what stands out

**What this is.** A distributor-owned, multi-brand system of record for Indian FMCG distribution, sold by subscription to the distributor, free for retailers. Not a brand DMS (Botree, FieldAssist, Shikhar serve the manufacturer), not a billing package (Marg, Vyapar, Busy stop at the invoice), not a marketplace (Udaan, Jumbotail, ONDC compete with the distributor). Nobody sells the distributor a system he owns across brands at SMB prices (R01 §1, R04 §4.3, R10 §2.11).

**Conversion thesis (from C; adopted as the pilot exit criterion).** Tarsun pays when three things are visibly true within two weeks of the parallel run: (1) inbound stock enters by photo with no typing except the blind gate count — no Indian DMS or billing product scans purchase invoices (R01 §4); (2) the physical pending-bills file is on his phone: per-retailer ledger, ageing 0-30/31-60/61-90/90+, UPI QR with his own VPA on every bill, WhatsApp reminders with the PDF (R09 §8, R10 §3); (3) his CA gets Tally XML that imports clean, unasked (R02 §6). Everything else is retention and is sequenced accordingly.
