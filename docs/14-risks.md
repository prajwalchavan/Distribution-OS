# Risks and mitigations

| #   | Risk                                                                                                            | L / I                        | Mitigation                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Solo schedule slip; 24 weeks is P50                                                                             | High / High                  | Dependency-ordered slices with acceptance tests; explicit cut line; no feature added without removing one; EAS Update post-launch; a date range for Tarsun, not a date                             |
| 2   | Sync bugs wedge devices or lose money rows                                                                      | Medium / Severe              | Never 4xx from upload; `sync_ops` unique + ledger idempotency indexes; GPS off the PowerSync queue; beat-scoped 90-day streams; replay tests; airplane-mode Maestro flow                           |
| 3   | Purchase price leaks to a salesperson                                                                           | Low / Severe                 | Separate cost table with role RLS; sell-side views in streams; CI role-leak dump; audit log on cost reads                                                                                          |
| 4   | Ledger drift or double posting                                                                                  | Medium / Severe              | Same-transaction balances with `CHECK`; unique idempotency keys on ledger rows; nightly re-derivation with alarm; compensating entries only; deferred journal trigger; restore drill with checksum |
| 5   | Docint accuracy on Indian layouts, pencil marks, Hindi annotations                                              | Medium / High                | Eval set from week 1, scored before enabling; engine chosen by eval; validators and QR facts route, not confidence; human commit; measured manual-entry hatch; per-supplier profiles               |
| 6   | Store rejection for background location; OEM battery killers                                                    | Medium / Medium              | Trip-scoped tracking with FGS notification; declaration + video in week 21; Transistorsoft fallback; "last point n min ago" visible to the owner                                                   |
| 7   | CA rejects the system (voucher mismatch, masters, free-qty convention)                                          | Medium / High                | Paisa-exact Round Off; masters before vouchers; per-tenant toggles; dry run against a restored backup; file export before connector                                                                |
| 8   | Brand-mandated DMS keeps the distributor double-keying                                                          | High / High                  | Brand-DMS ingestion with commit in the pilot; per-brand exports; Tally-shaped read endpoint                                                                                                        |
| 9   | Retailer adoption near zero                                                                                     | High / Medium                | WhatsApp invoices/POD/receipts from day one; PWA before native; in-beat onboarding; self-placed share measured weekly; native only on data                                                         |
| 10  | Messaging, OTP and LLM costs erode the subscription                                                             | Medium / Medium              | Per-tenant cost lines from week 1; WhatsApp quota + metering; in-window replies; WhatsApp auth + long sessions; tight schema; spend alarms; ₹0.5/order worst case budgeted                         |
| 11  | Stack freshness (Nest 12 one week old, Expo 57 New-Arch-only, Drizzle pre-1.0, Better Auth, oRPC, PowerSync v2) | Medium / Medium              | Week-1 compatibility spike with Nest 11 fallback; every version pinned; quarterly upgrade window; ADR per vendor with an exit note                                                                 |
| 12  | PowerSync region, licence or business change                                                                    | Medium / High                | Region verified week 1; Cloud unless residency forces self-host; `packages/offline` is the only PowerSync code; server-authoritative design keeps logic in Postgres + Nest                         |
| 13  | Primary saturation (pg-boss + LISTEN/NOTIFY + replication slot)                                                 | Low at pilot / Medium at 100 | t4g.small floor; `max_slot_wal_keep_size` + slot-lag alarm; fan-out to Valkey/BullMQ above ~10% CPU; replica and `(tenant_id, occurred_at)` partitioning at 100                                    |
| 14  | Global master polluted by tenant proposals                                                                      | Medium / Medium              | `proposed` status, curation queue, `merged_into` merges before tenant #3                                                                                                                           |
| 15  | Compliance drift (GST 2.0 rates, EWB churn, DPDP by 13 May 2027, e-invoice at ₹5 crore)                         | Medium / Medium              | Dated `hsn_rates`; MRP per lot; original-rate credit notes; IRN-ready model; EWB fields kept, not depended on; notices, retention and breach runbook in the pilot                                  |
