# Glossary

- **Primary / secondary / tertiary sales** — company->distributor / distributor->retailer / retailer->consumer.
- **C&F (CFA)** — carrying & forwarding agent; holds brand stock in a state depot, does not own it.
- **Super stockist (SS)** — buys from company/CFA, sells to several distributors in a district; **sub-stockist / sub-distributor** — buys from a distributor to serve an outlying pocket.
- **RS** — redistribution stockist (HUL's term for distributor). **DB** — distributor (industry shorthand).
- **GT / MT** — general trade (kirana) / modern trade (chains).
- **Beat**, **PJP**, **DSR (daily sales report)** — see section 2. **DSM / DSR / ISR** — distributor's salesman; **TSI / SO / ASM** — company field staff. **PSR** — pre-sales rep (books orders, no stock).
- **PCR / strike rate** — productive calls / calls; **LPC** — lines per call; **ECO** — effectively covered outlets; **bill-cut** — a retailer billed at least once in the period.
- **MRP, PTR/RLP, PTD/DLP, landing price** — see section 4. **Margin on MRP vs on PTD** — two bases; store which.
- **Case / box / outer / inner / pc** — pack hierarchy; **CS1** — SAP UoM code for case; "x 90", "_120" — case size suffixes in item names.
- **Scheme** — any trade promotion; **primary scheme**, **secondary scheme**, **QPS**, **slab/TOD**, **display scheme**, **FOC / free qty / 10+1**, **CD (cash discount)**, **secondary discount** (the discount the distributor passes to the retailer on the company's behalf), **price protection / rate difference**.
- **Claim** — distributor's request to the company to be reimbursed for a scheme, damage, expiry, display or rate difference; settled by **credit note (CN)** (tax CN reduces GST; financial/commercial CN does not), by **in-bill adjustment**, or by free stock.
- **GRN** — goods receipt note (record of what physically arrived vs invoice); **LR / GR** — lorry receipt / goods receipt issued by transporter (invoice D); **POD** — proof of delivery; **RTV** — return to vendor; **L&D** — leakage & damage claim; **SLOB** — slow-moving and obsolete stock; **FEFO** — first-expiry-first-out.
- **Load sheet / loading sheet** — per-trip manifest signed by driver; **van godown** — vehicle treated as a stock location; **EOD settlement** — stock + cash reconciliation at trip end.
- **Bill-to-bill** — retailer pays the previous bill when the next is delivered; **udhaar/khata** — running credit; **DSO** — days sales outstanding; **aging** — 0-30/31-60/61-90/90+.
- **URP** — unregistered person (retailer without GSTIN); **IRN/QR** — e-invoice reference; **EWB** — e-way bill; **HSN** — tariff code; **GST 2.0** — 22 Sept 2025 rate rationalisation.
- **Dumping / forced primary** — company pushing stock beyond secondary demand; **closing stock** — distributor stock at period end (the number companies watch).
