# R02 — Indian billing/ERP software FMCG distributors actually use: teardown + coexistence formats

Date: 2026-09-04. Scope: Marg ERP, Busy, TallyPrime, Vyapar, Logic ERP, Gofrugal, Billdev, ERP Group, Zoho Books/Inventory, myBillBook, Swipe. Plus: which product printed retailer invoice "F" (Bill No GL/1686), and exactly how a new system pushes sales invoices into Tally so the accountant keeps working.

Everything below is from vendor help centres, pricing pages, review sites and developer docs fetched today; items I could not verify are marked **[unverified]**. Prices are ex-GST unless stated.

---

## 1. Bottom line (read this if nothing else)

1. **Invoice F was almost certainly printed by Marg ERP** (desktop, Windows). Marg's own help pages show the default GST invoice column set as `HSN | Free | Qty | Scheme | Disc | Rate | MRP`, its standard header labels as `Bill No | Date | Code | Party`, a printable `Due Date` header field, and a default format named **"GST Local"** — which explains the `GL/` series prefix. See section 2.
2. **Tally is the accountant's system of record, not the distributor's billing tool.** Every billing product in this list (Marg, Busy, Vyapar, myBillBook, Swipe, Zoho) ships a "export to Tally" path, and all of them do it the same way: **two Tally XML files (masters + vouchers) or an HTTP POST of the same XML to TallyPrime's port 9000**. That is the format we must emit. Section 6 gives a working voucher skeleton and the operational rules.
3. **Marg is the incumbent to displace for a Thane FMCG distributor.** Its feature depth on schemes is real (free qty by item/batch/party/date, "net scheme" amount conversion, 9 price lists, PTR/PTS, operator-wise hiding of cost/margin), which is why it survives despite 2.5/5 Trustpilot and endemic "reseller ping-pong" support complaints. We do not need to match all of it; we need to match the ~12 behaviours in section 4 and beat it on mobile, multi-user roles and support.
4. **The cheap mobile-first apps (Vyapar, myBillBook, Swipe) do not model distributor schemes** (free qty exists as a line, "scheme %" does not; no case/piece dual UoM with per-unit rates; weak role control). They matter as the retailer's tool and as a price anchor (₹3.4k–7.5k/yr), not as a distributor competitor.
5. **Minimum interop set to coexist on day 1:** (a) Tally XML export of sales/credit-note/receipt vouchers + party ledgers + stock items; (b) Tally HTTP push via a small Windows connector; (c) CSV/Excel import of item, party and opening-outstanding masters (this is how Marg/Busy users will migrate — both export any report to Excel); (d) Marg/Busy native XML import is **not** worth building — read their Excel exports instead.

Quick confirmation for the founder: ask Tarsun which desktop program prints the Alan's/Masti Oye bills — if it is Marg, the item master already holds MRP/PTR/scheme data we can pull via Marg's Excel export.

Implication for Document Intelligence: build a **Marg GST-Local invoice parser profile** (column order above, `Qty`/`Free` in pieces, `Rate` = per-piece pre-tax, `Scheme` = %, `Disc Amt` = ₹, `Taxable` = Qty×Rate less scheme less disc, tax split SGST/CGST). Marg also prints `Pack`/packing and batch/expiry columns in pharma formats; FMCG users typically switch those off.

### 3.2 Marg ERP — what makes it sticky (and what to copy)

Marg's public feature list is the best available spec of "what a scheme-heavy Indian distributor expects". Verbatim items worth designing against ([key_features](https://margcompusoft.com/key_features.html)):

- Free schemes: "Party Master, Item, Batch, Date wise scheme"; "Free scheme calculation on Item, Batch, Manual, Lot bases"; "Provision to calculate free scheme in amount if Free Quantity <1"; "Free goods in Amount (Net Scheme) on .45*Rate, .50*Rate, .45/.50*(Rate*Deal/(Deal+Free))"; "Tax on free goods Company/Item/Batch wise option"; "Other free provision i.e. Lux Shop free with 10kg Marg Aata" (cross-item free); "Operator wise extra scheme issue allowed/disallowed".
- Discounts: "Bill wise 4 Discounts in percentage", "Item wise 2 Discount in percentage", "2 Volume Discounts on Per Unit/Lumpsum bases", "Quantity wise Discounts", "Maximum discount limits Company/Item wise", "Operator wise maximum discount", "Discount Lessby system i.e. 10% Disc – 2% Lessby: print 10%, calculate 9.8%".
- Rates: "9 Price Lists, Party wise Special Rates, Minimum Rates, Pick Rates from Last Delivery, Net Rate and Lot Rate"; "Price to Retailer / Price to Stockist provision"; "Party wise Add in Rate provision e.g. Rate = Rate+%, Rate+PurchRate*%, Rate-MRP*%/100"; "Operator wise sale rate decrease upto %".
- Units: "Stocks & Billing in Multi Unit eg 10 Bag 12 Kg"; item master has Unit-1 (small, e.g. PCS) / Unit-2 (large) with a conversion factor and a rate stored per unit; bills can show either unit ([multi-unit help](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/inventory/2001/1/How-we-can-enable-multi)).
- Credit: "Party wise separate credit limits in Amount, Bills & Due Days"; "If party cross his credit limit then Indicate, Follow Strictly, Stop Billing"; "Operator wise powers to cross with password"; "Send direct SMS if party cross his primary / credit limit".
- Margin secrecy: "Default/Operator wise Margins, Cost, Purchase Rate & Deals display or not"; "Show/Disable margin, cost, purchase rate, purchase deal on Tab Key". (Marg already solves the Vyapar role-leak that made staff quit; we must at least match it.)
- Returns: "Sales Return, Breakage/Expiry Return, Replacement, Price Difference Dr/Cr Note in Sales Bill" — four distinct return reasons, each posting differently.
- Purchase import: "Purchase Import (E-Milan) … Import from CSV, Excel, DBF, etc format (Other software)" — i.e. Marg users already expect to load supplier invoices from soft copies; our photo→LLM path replaces this for suppliers who only give paper.

Mobile: eOrder (salesman ordering with live stock, outstanding, PDC, offline cart), eRetail (retailer self-ordering + Marg Pay payments), eOwner (dashboard) ([eOrder page](https://margcompusoft.com/eorder_salesman_order_app.html), [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.marg.newmargorder)). These are bolt-ons to a desktop core; sync is file/cloud based, not real time.

Real complaints (Trustpilot 2.5/5 from 111 reviews; Capterra 3.2/5 from 55): "After the sale they are not responding to emails" (Aug 2025); "everyone keeps forwarding the issue to someone else" (May 2026); "customers should understand the dependency created by the dealer network" (Aug 2026); "exploit its customer by first selling their software and after that they make money in renewal time" (Nov 2025); "Charging RS 5500+GST but website clearly mentions RS 4500"; "Printed 4 different bills with the same bill number" (Feb 2021); "Not cloud based/SaaS … many difficulties"; "MARG only targeted pharma business" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/margcompusoft.com), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/151832/Erp-Software-9/reviews/)). Pricing is sales-led, reseller-quoted, one-time + AMC, and Marg explicitly says software bought outside authorised partners gets no support ([margerpmaharashtra](https://margerpmaharashtra.in/marg-erp-price/)).

### 3.3 Busy — accountant-friendly, FMCG pack is real

Busy's FMCG page lists exactly the distributor vocabulary: "Auto Scheme Apply (e.g., Buy 10 Get 1)", "Discount Slab System (e.g., Upto 10, 20)", "Add Free Quantity", "Batch-wise Expiry Tracking", "Expired Batch Restrictions", "Multiple units for products – Carton, Box, Pcs", "Van Management for Routes", "Stock Transfer (Godown to Van)", "Mobile App for Salesman", "Aging Report (30/60/90 Days)", "Auto-Generate e-Way Bill & e-Invoice" ([busy.in/fmcg](https://busy.in/accounting-software/fmcg/)). Import/export: `Administration → Data Export Import → Import Vouchers from Excel` (user-defined column mapping) and `Data Export/Import (XML)` for masters + vouchers in Busy's own XML ([FAQ](https://busy.in/faqs/how-do-i-importexport-data-in-busy-answerid-67737/)). Busy states "Currently, BUSY does not have any default option for integration of any third party software" — custom via channel partner; a paid REST API exists through partners ([integration FAQ](https://busy.in/faqs/third-party-services/integration/1/), [BusyNotify](https://busynotify.in/solutions/custom-apis)). Complaints are milder than Marg: dated UI, hard to find reports, SMS agent broken, limited backup targets ([Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/reviews/busy-accounting-software)).

### 3.4 TallyPrime — the target, not the competitor

Distributors' CAs live in Tally. Relevant facts: Rel 4.0 added Excel import of masters/vouchers and WhatsApp send; Rel 5.0 "Connected GST"; Rel 6.0 bank-statement automation; Rel 7.0 JSON import/export; Rel 7.1 (current) flexible invoice print templates, HSN/SAC validation ([release-wise](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tallyprime-features-release-wise/)). Free goods are modelled as **Actual Qty ≠ Billed Qty** ("Rate is charged on the basis of Billed Quantity but the stock is updated on the basis of Actual quantity; the difference is FREE") after enabling F11 → "Use separate Actual and Billed Quantity columns" ([Tally help](https://help.tallysolutions.com/docs/te9rel50/Voucher_Entry/Advanced_Voucher_Entry/Using_different_Actual_and_Billed_Quantities.htm), [TallyPrimeBook](https://tallyprimebook.com/sales-with-free-items-different-actual-and-billed-quantities-in-sale-invoice-in-tallyprime-rel-4/)). Excel import guidance: "import 50,000 to 1 Lakh transactions at a time"; a 14-digit batch number imported from Excel "displays a negative value" (bug to avoid — send batch names as strings via XML) ([Import FAQ](https://help.tallysolutions.com/import-data-faq/)). Pricing: Silver ₹22,500 / Gold ₹67,500 perpetual; rental 8,100 / 24,300 per year incl. TSS; TSS renewal 4,500 / 13,500 ([markitsolutions](https://www.markitsolutions.in/pricing/), [Tally TCO](https://tallysolutions.com/business-guides/tallyprime-total-cost-of-ownership-india/)).

### 3.5 Vyapar, myBillBook, Swipe — the ₹4k/yr tier

All three: mobile-first, GST invoices, khata/reminders, WhatsApp share, free-qty per line, batch/expiry on paid tiers, a "Tally export". None has a scheme engine, dual-UoM pricing, operator-level cost hiding, or beat/route logic. Vyapar's documented failures are the ones in CONTEXT.md plus: "even when using Salesman options, employees can still see purchase price and stock information" (App Store), "predefined roles lack flexibility", "cannot add customers sharing identical names", "requires manual mouse clicks instead of keyboard shortcuts", "WhatsApp invoice … sent as blank files" ([Trustpilot 3.6/5](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/vyaparapp.in), [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/vyapar-billing-accounting/id6478382307?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)). myBillBook: "Tally compatibility could be improved", "lacks offline billing", "duplicates/triplicates printing" ([Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/reviews/mybillbook-accounting-software), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/202732/FloBooks/reviews/)). These are the apps a _retailer_ may already use for their own shop — relevant for the retailer app's "outstanding" view, not for the distributor.

### 3.6 Logic ERP, Gofrugal, Billdev, ERP Group

## 4. Behaviours every incumbent has — the bar for parity

1. **Free qty is a separate column on the line**, not a second line at ₹0 (Marg `Free`, Busy "Add Free Quantity", Tally Actual vs Billed). Stock ledger must decrement Qty+Free; revenue uses Qty only; a config decides whether GST applies on free goods ("Tax on free goods Company/Item/Batch wise option" in Marg).
2. **Two percentage discounts per line + up to four per bill** (Marg), or scheme slab + discount (Busy). Invoice F shows `Scheme` (%) and `Disc Amt` (₹) side by side. Our pricing engine already stacks schemes; make sure the _printed_ invoice can show scheme % and discount ₹ as separate columns, and that `Taxable = Qty×Rate − scheme − disc` matches to the paisa.
3. **Qty-slab rates and schemes** ("Qty wise Rate/Scheme": buy ≥N pieces → different rate/free) ([Marg](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/rate-and-discount-master/2205/1/null)).
4. **Dual UoM with a rate per unit** and printing "2 Case" under a 180-pc line (invoice A) — case size lives on the SKU (90, 120, 24…), rate is per piece for retail, per case for wholesale.
5. **MRP on every line** (statutory for packaged goods; Marg/Busy/Logic all print it) and MRP-wise batches (same SKU, two MRPs in stock at once after a price change).
6. **Batch + expiry + near-expiry prompt at billing** and **breakage/expiry returns as a distinct return reason** (Marg, Busy, Logic).
7. **Credit control at three levels** — amount, number of open bills, days — with modes "indicate / follow strictly / stop billing" and an operator override password (Marg). Our approvals queue is the natural home for "override".
8. **Party code** printed on invoices (retailers quote it on the phone) and **Due Date** derived from party credit days.
9. **Bill series** per sale type (local/interstate/retail/cash) with prefixes; GST rule: unique sequential numbering per series per FY.
10. **Operator-wise hiding of cost/margin/purchase deal** (Marg) — CONTEXT's hard test.
11. **Outstanding views by party / area / route / salesman with ageing buckets**, printable statements, SMS/WhatsApp reminders (Marg lists 24 outstanding formats; Busy 30/60/90 ageing).
12. **Export anything to Excel + export to Tally** — the accountant's expectation.

## 6. Concretely: pushing our sales invoices into Tally so the accountant keeps working

### 6.1 Architecture

- Tally is a Windows desktop app on the CA's or the distributor's PC. Its XML gateway answers **only while TallyPrime is open with the company loaded**, on the port set under F1 (Help) → Settings → Advanced Configuration → "Enable HTTP server / port 9000" ([Tally XML integration](https://help.tallysolutions.com/xml-integration/), [pre-requisites](https://help.tallysolutions.com/pre-requisites-for-integrations/), [Appycodes 2026 guide](https://appycodes.dev/blog/tally-integration-tdl-guide-2026/)).
- Therefore: ship a **tiny Windows "Tally Connector"** (a signed .NET/Go service, ~5 MB) that runs on the same PC/LAN as Tally, polls our API for pending vouchers for that tenant, POSTs them to `localhost:9000`, and reports the `IMPORTRESULT` back. No inbound ports on the customer network; works with Tally-on-cloud partners only if they expose port 9000 to the connector host.
- **Always also offer the file path**: "Download Tally XML (masters + vouchers) for date range" → the CA imports via Gateway of Tally → Import Data → Masters/Transactions. This is what Vyapar, myBillBook, Zoho and Marg do today, so it needs zero training ([Vyapar guide](https://vyaparapp.in/guides/how-to-import-and-export-vyapar-app-data-into-tally), [Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/in/invoice/help/marketplace-extensions/export-tally-xml-format.html), [myBillBook](https://mybillbook.in/s/features/data-export-to-tally/)).

### 6.2 One-time mapping (per tenant, done by the CA in our UI)

| Our concept                | Tally master                                                                                                                                                                                  | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Retailer (tenant-scoped)   | Ledger under **Sundry Debtors**                                                                                                                                                               | Name must match exactly (case, spaces). Store `tally_ledger_name` on the retailer; default = "<Shop name> – <code>". Push `GSTREGISTRATIONTYPE` = Regular / Unregistered / Composition, `PARTYGSTIN`, `LEDSTATENAME`/`STATENAME`, mailing address, `BILLCREDITPERIOD` |
| Sales revenue              | Ledger under **Sales Accounts** — usually one per GST rate ("Sales @5%", "Sales @12%", "Sales @18%") or a single "Sales" with item-level tax                                                  | Ask the CA which convention their company uses; both are common                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| CGST / SGST / IGST output  | Ledgers under **Duties & Taxes** with `GSTDUTYHEAD` Central/State/Integrated Tax                                                                                                              | Names vary ("Output CGST", "CGST 2.5%"); map once                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Round-off                  | Ledger under Indirect Expenses/Incomes, "Round Off"                                                                                                                                           | Post the paisa residual here so the voucher balances exactly — "If rounding leaves a voucher a paisa out, Tally rejects the whole thing" ([Appycodes](https://appycodes.dev/blog/tally-integration-tdl-guide-2026/))                                                  |
| Product (global master)    | Stock Item, `BASEUNITS` = Pcs; optional compound unit "Case of 90 Pcs" via `ADDITIONALUNITS`/`CONVERSION`; `GSTDETAILS.LIST` with `HSNCODE` and `RATEDETAILS.LIST` (Central/State/Integrated) | Tally item names are per company, so keep `tally_item_name` per tenant. | Warehouse                  | Godown                                                                                                                                                                                        | Default "Main Location"                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

| Invoice series (`GL/1686`) | Voucher Type "Sales" (or a custom child type per series) + `VOUCHERNUMBER`                                                                                                                    | Tally accepts any string; set the CA's voucher type to manual numbering so our numbers stick                                                                                                                                                                          |

### 6.3 Voucher shape (sales invoice with free qty, scheme %, discount ₹, batches, CGST/SGST)

Based on Tally's official sample ([help.tallysolutions.com/sample-xml](https://help.tallysolutions.com/sample-xml/)), the Case Study request/response ([case_study_1](https://help.tallysolutions.com/article/DeveloperReference/integration-capabilities/case_study_1.htm)) and tag names in the public template. Sign rule: **debits are negative, credits positive; `ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE=Yes` on the debit (party) entry** ([TrulyInvoice tag guide](https://www.trulyinvoice.com/blog/standard-tally-xml-tags-for-sales-vouchers)).

```xml
<ENVELOPE>
 <HEADER><TALLYREQUEST>Import Data</TALLYREQUEST></HEADER>
 <BODY><IMPORTDATA>
  <REQUESTDESC>
   <REPORTNAME>Vouchers</REPORTNAME>
   <STATICVARIABLES><SVCURRENTCOMPANY>Tarsun Enterprises</SVCURRENTCOMPANY></STATICVARIABLES>
  </REQUESTDESC>
  <REQUESTDATA><TALLYMESSAGE xmlns:UDF="TallyUDF">
   <VOUCHER VCHTYPE="Sales" ACTION="Create" OBJVIEW="Invoice Voucher View">
    <DATE>20260904</DATE>
    <GUID>dos-tarsun-inv-GL-1686</GUID>              <!-- our stable id; prevents double-post -->
    <VOUCHERTYPENAME>Sales</VOUCHERTYPENAME>
    <VOUCHERNUMBER>GL/1686</VOUCHERNUMBER>
    <REFERENCE>SO-000231</REFERENCE>
    <PARTYLEDGERNAME>Shree Shakti Provision Store - 14023</PARTYLEDGERNAME>
    <BASICBUYERNAME>Shree Shakti Provision Store</BASICBUYERNAME>
    <PARTYGSTIN></PARTYGSTIN>
    <GSTREGISTRATIONTYPE>Unregistered/Consumer</GSTREGISTRATIONTYPE>
    <STATENAME>Maharashtra</STATENAME>
    <PLACEOFSUPPLY>Maharashtra</PLACEOFSUPPLY>
    <BASICDUEDATEOFPYMT>0 Days</BASICDUEDATEOFPYMT>
    <ISINVOICE>Yes</ISINVOICE>
    <PERSISTEDVIEW>Invoice Voucher View</PERSISTEDVIEW>
    <NARRATION>DOS invoice GL/1686 [dos:inv_01J...]</NARRATION>

    <!-- Party: debit (negative), bill-wise reference for outstanding -->
    <LEDGERENTRIES.LIST>
     <LEDGERNAME>Shree Shakti Provision Store - 14023</LEDGERNAME>
     <ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE>Yes</ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE><ISPARTYLEDGER>Yes</ISPARTYLEDGER>
     <AMOUNT>-1200.00</AMOUNT>
     <BILLALLOCATIONS.LIST>
      <NAME>GL/1686</NAME><BILLTYPE>New Ref</BILLTYPE>
      <BILLCREDITPERIOD>0 Days</BILLCREDITPERIOD><AMOUNT>-1200.00</AMOUNT>
     </BILLALLOCATIONS.LIST>
    </LEDGERENTRIES.LIST>

    <!-- Line: 300 pcs billed + 30 free; rate 3.81/pc; scheme 5% + disc ₹20 -->
    <ALLINVENTORYENTRIES.LIST>
     <STOCKITEMNAME>Alans Bhujia 5 Rs (x90)</STOCKITEMNAME>
     <ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE>No</ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE>
     <RATE>3.81/Pcs</RATE>
     <DISCOUNT>5</DISCOUNT>                          <!-- % ; needs 'separate discount column' on in Tally -->
     <ACTUALQTY>330 Pcs</ACTUALQTY>                  <!-- stock out incl. free -->
     <BILLEDQTY>300 Pcs</BILLEDQTY>                  <!-- revenue basis -->
     <AMOUNT>1065.85</AMOUNT>                        <!-- 300*3.81 = 1143.00 less 5% = 1085.85 less 20.00 -->
     <BATCHALLOCATIONS.LIST>
      <GODOWNNAME>Main Location</GODOWNNAME><BATCHNAME>N526205</BATCHNAME>
      <ACTUALQTY>330 Pcs</ACTUALQTY><BILLEDQTY>300 Pcs</BILLEDQTY><AMOUNT>1065.85</AMOUNT>
     </BATCHALLOCATIONS.LIST>
     <ACCOUNTINGALLOCATIONS.LIST>
      <LEDGERNAME>Sales @5%</LEDGERNAME><ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE>No</ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE><AMOUNT>1065.85</AMOUNT>
     </ACCOUNTINGALLOCATIONS.LIST>
    </ALLINVENTORYENTRIES.LIST>

    <!-- Taxes: credits (positive) -->
    <LEDGERENTRIES.LIST><LEDGERNAME>Output CGST</LEDGERNAME><ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE>No</ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE><AMOUNT>26.65</AMOUNT></LEDGERENTRIES.LIST>
    <LEDGERENTRIES.LIST><LEDGERNAME>Output SGST</LEDGERNAME><ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE>No</ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE><AMOUNT>26.65</AMOUNT></LEDGERENTRIES.LIST>
    <!-- Round off so Dr == Cr to the paisa -->
    <LEDGERENTRIES.LIST><LEDGERNAME>Round Off</LEDGERNAME><ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE>No</ISDEEMEDPOSITIVE><AMOUNT>80.85</AMOUNT></LEDGERENTRIES.LIST>
   </VOUCHER>
  </TALLYMESSAGE></REQUESTDATA>
 </IMPORTDATA></BODY>
</ENVELOPE>
```

(The numbers above are illustrative; in production the round-off line is the true residual, typically ≤ ₹0.50. The ₹20 flat discount must either be folded into the line `AMOUNT` as shown or posted to a separate "Discount" ledger — pick per CA.)

Response to parse ([case_study_1](https://help.tallysolutions.com/article/DeveloperReference/integration-capabilities/case_study_1.htm)):

```xml
<ENVELOPE><HEADER><VERSION>1</VERSION><STATUS>1</STATUS></HEADER>
 <BODY><DATA><IMPORTRESULT>
  <CREATED>1</CREATED><ALTERED>0</ALTERED><LASTVCHID>119</LASTVCHID>
  <LASTMID>0</LASTMID><COMBINED>0</COMBINED><IGNORED>0</IGNORED><ERRORS>0</ERRORS>
 </IMPORTRESULT></DATA></BODY></ENVELOPE>
```

`ERRORS>0` comes with `<LINEERROR>` text such as "Voucher totals do not match!", "Referenced master 'X' does not exist", "Date … is out of range", "Voucher Type … does not exist", "Can't mix Stock Items and Accounting Entries" ([TrulyInvoice error decoder](https://www.trulyinvoice.com/blog/resolve-tally-excel-xml-import-errors)). Store `LASTVCHID` + `LASTMID` against our invoice as the Tally reference.

### 6.4 Operating rules (what makes it reliable)

1. **Masters before vouchers, every run.** Send `ID=All Masters` with `ACTION="Create"` for any ledger/item/unit/godown first; Tally treats an existing name as alter (use `ACTION="Alter"` explicitly if updating). Missing masters "fail silently" when only vouchers are sent ([Appycodes](https://appycodes.dev/blog/tally-integration-tdl-guide-2026/)).
2. **Idempotency.** Keep a per-tenant sync ledger keyed by our invoice id → (Tally `GUID` we assigned, `LASTVCHID`, status). Never re-POST a `Create` for a synced invoice; corrections go as `ACTION="Alter"` with the same `GUID`, cancellations as `ACTION="Cancel"` or a Credit Note per CA preference. Tally's import supports `IMPORTDUPS` = `@@DUPCOMBINE` / ignore / modify behaviour ([case_study_1](https://help.tallysolutions.com/article/DeveloperReference/integration-capabilities/case_study_1.htm)).
3. **Balance to the paisa** per voucher; put residual in Round Off; use 2-decimal strings; no thousands separators; dates `YYYYMMDD` inside the company's financial year ("Date … is out of range" otherwise).
4. **Quantities carry the unit symbol** (`300 Pcs`, `3.81/Pcs`). Unit symbols must exist as Tally Unit masters (Tally also wants a `GSTREPUOM`/`REPORTINGUQCNAME` like `PCS-PIECES` for GSTR-1 HSN summary since Rel 3.0 — seen in the template at [ShwetaSoftwares](https://github.com/ShwetaSoftwares/excel-to-tally-templates/blob/master/Master-StockItemMaster-xml-tags.xml)).
5. **Free qty** → `ACTUALQTY` > `BILLEDQTY` (requires F11 "separate Actual and Billed" in the CA's company). If the CA refuses to enable it, fallback: a second inventory line for the free qty at rate 0 — flag this as a per-tenant setting.
6. **Scheme % and Disc ₹** → `DISCOUNT` (percentage, requires "separate discount column in invoices") or pre-net the `AMOUNT`. Per-tenant setting again; default to pre-netting because it works on every Tally configuration.
7. **GST**: for intra-state, Output CGST/SGST ledgers; interstate, Output IGST; unregistered retailers still get `GSTREGISTRATIONTYPE=Unregistered/Consumer` and Tally files them under B2CS. Sales ledgers per rate keep the CA's GSTR-1 clean.
8. **Credit notes** (sales returns) → `VOUCHERTYPENAME=Credit Note` with `BILLALLOCATIONS` of type `Agst Ref` naming the original invoice; **receipts** (cash/UPI collections from the delivery app) → `Receipt` voucher: Dr Cash/UPI bank ledger, Cr party with `Agst Ref` to the bills settled (this is what keeps the CA's bill-wise outstanding in sync with our khata).
9. **Batching and pacing.** POST in batches of 50–200 vouchers; Tally is single-threaded; a large import locks the UI. Run at night or on the CA's command ("Sync now"), never continuously during billing hours.
10. **Dry run first**: import into a restored backup of the CA's company; compare Tally's Sales Register total for the day to ours before going live ("always run the first import against a restored backup" — [Appycodes](https://appycodes.dev/blog/tally-integration-tdl-guide-2026/)).
11. **Read-back for migration**: `TALLYREQUEST=Export`, `TYPE=Collection`, inline `<TDL><COLLECTION><TYPE>Ledger</TYPE><FETCH>NAME,PARENT,LEDSTATENAME,PARTYGSTIN,CLOSINGBALANCE</FETCH>` to pull debtors with balances; `Bills` collection for bill-wise outstanding; `StockItem` for items — used by the migration playbook's "reference data first, outstanding reconciled with human checkpoint" step.

### 6.5 What we can _not_ do through Tally (so we don't promise it)

- Tally has no scheme engine; scheme reimbursement claims to brands, beat/route, salesman incentives stay in our system. Tally gets the accounting effect only.
- Tally is not real-time for the CA; ODBC is deprecated in TallyPrime 4.0+ in favour of HTTP ([Terra Insight](https://www.terra-insight.com/insights/tally-prime-reconciliation-automation-india/)).
- Tally on Cloud (partner-hosted) may not expose port 9000; the file path is the fallback.

## 7. Recommendations

1. **Confirm invoice F's software with Tarsun** (one WhatsApp question). If Marg: get their Excel exports of Item master, Party master, Outstanding (Marg → Reports → Outstanding → export Excel) and use them as the first three migration fixtures.
2. **Build the Tally XML exporter in month 1** (file pair), the **Windows connector in month 2** (HTTP push + `IMPORTRESULT` handling + sync ledger). This is the single feature that lets the CA say yes.
3. **Invoice print template must be Marg-shaped** for the pilot: `HSN | Product | Qty | Free | MRP | Rate | Scheme % | Disc ₹ | Taxable | SGST% | CGST% | Net`, header `Bill No | Date | Due Date | Party Code`, `ORIGINAL/DUPLICATE/TRIPLICATE` captions, UPI QR. Retailers and the CA already read this layout.
4. **Pricing-engine additions** (recommended changes, not contradictions of CONTEXT): (a) qty-slab schemes; (b) "net scheme" option (free converted to ₹ when free qty < 1 or brand wants amount); (c) per-tenant flag "GST on free goods"; (d) credit limits in amount / bills / days with indicate–strict–stop modes and owner override in the approvals queue; (e) four return reasons (saleable return, breakage/expiry, replacement, price difference) each with its own posting.
5. **Import profiles** to write: Tally XML masters (ledgers/items), Marg Excel (item/party/outstanding), Busy Excel, generic CSV. Do not reverse-engineer Marg DBF or Busy XML.
6. **Pricing anchor**: distributors already pay ₹8–26k one-time + ₹2–5k/yr AMC (Marg) or ₹5–16.5k (Busy) plus ₹8–24k/yr for Tally. A subscription around ₹1,000–2,500/month per distributor is within their existing spend envelope; per-device licences (Vyapar's model) are actively resented.
7. **Support is the wedge**: the loudest Marg complaints are support ping-pong and renewal surprises. A public price, in-app chat, and "we fix it or you don't pay this month" is a differentiator no incumbent offers.
