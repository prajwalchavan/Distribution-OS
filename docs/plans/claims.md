## 1. Purpose and boundaries

Claims is the back-office ledger of money the **manufacturer owes the distributor**: company-funded scheme spend already given away on invoices, damaged/expired goods the brand takes back, inbound shortages and rate differences. It owns `claims`, `claim_lines`, `claim_evidence`, `claim_statements` and the new `claim_settlements`; it reads everything else through the owning module's exported service — `PricingService`/schemes (`claimable`, `funding_source`, `claim_channel`, `source_ref`, `claim_window_days`), `BillingService` (`invoices`, `invoice_lines.applied_rules`, `credit_notes` + lines), `ProcurementService` (`inbound_discrepancies`, and marking them `claimed`), `InventoryService` (`stock_ledger` rows with reason `damage` / `expiry_writeoff` into the damaged location, `stock_lots` batch/expiry/case_size), `TenantCatalogService` (`tenant_brands.claim_channel`, `return_policies`, `suppliers`, `tenant_product_costs` for the valuation basis), `ReceivablesService` (posts every journal entry) and `IntegrationsService` (enqueues the `export_jobs` row for the claim sheet). It writes nothing outside its five tables except through those services; it emits `ClaimSubmitted` / `ClaimSettled` / `ClaimRejected` / `ClaimWrittenOff` / `ClaimStatementRequested` outbox events for the worker and notifications.
Everything here is **back office only** (owner / manager / accountant / system): `claim_lines.rate_paise` on a damage line *is* purchase cost, so claims are served only by owner-service (:3001) and manager-service (:3002), never registered with `SyncRegistry`, and never in a PowerSync bucket (docs/17 §B security 54–57).
If `BillingModule` / `ReceivablesModule` / `IntegrationsModule` are still README stubs when this slice runs, add the exact read/write method to that module's service (signatures in §4 rule 17) rather than querying its tables from claims; a temporary direct read must carry a `// TODO(module-boundary)` comment and an `eslint-plugin-boundaries` exception, per docs/20 rule 12.

## 4. Domain rules and edge cases

1. **Money is integer paise, always.** `paise()`, `sum()`, `percentOf()`, `allocate()` from `@dos/domain`; no float ever touches `amount_paise`. `claims.claimed_paise` must equal `Σ claim_lines.amount_paise` of non-rejected lines after every mutation — recompute, never increment blindly.
2. **Quantities are integer pieces.** Case display for scheme lines uses the sell-side case size (`tenant_products.case_size_override ?? product_variants.default_case_size`, the `sellCaseSize()` rule of docs/17 §B); damage/expiry lines use the **lot's own** `stock_lots.case_size` (docs/17 A2) and store it in `claim_lines.case_size` so a claim sheet reprints "3 cs + 12 pcs" after the case size changes.
3. **IST everywhere.** `businessDate()` / `financialYear()` from `@dos/domain/calendar`. `period_from` / `period_to` are inclusive IST business dates; invoice matching compares the `invoice_date` **date** column, never a timestamp. Ageing days = `daysBetween(submitted_at IST date, asOf)`.
4. **Numbering only at submit.** `nextDocumentNumber(tx, 'CLAIM')` → `CLM-0001` from the seeded CLAIM series, per tenant per FY, ≤16 chars. A draft never has a number; a rejected or written-off claim **keeps** its number.
5. **States only through `claimMachine.next()`.** Never assign `status` by hand. An invalid transition surfaces as `TransitionError` → 409.
6. **Distributor-funded schemes are never claimable.** Only `schemes.claimable = true AND funding_source = 'company'` produce lines. `funding_source = 'distributor'` is our own promotion cost (booked to `SCHEME_EXPENSE` at invoice time) and claiming it would be fraud on the brand.
7. **`claim_channel` is frozen at open** from `tenant_brands.claim_channel`. `brand_dms` claims (Too Yumm on FieldAssist) are tracked, numbered, built and exported as a record only: **no journal entry ever**, and they are excluded from `claims.ageing` totals. Posting an accrual for them would double-count against the credit the brand gives inside its own DMS (docs/17 A1, ADR 0014).
8. **Cash-discount schemes are excluded** from the build even when flagged claimable: `reward_kind = 'cash_discount_pct'` is realised at receipt through `cash_discount_conditions` (ADR 0004), not given away on the invoice, so there is nothing to claim.
9. **One source, one claim.** `claim_lines_source_unique_idx` (partial, `status <> 'rejected'`) is the guarantee; the build must catch the conflict and count it in `skipped`, never crash. Rejecting a claim frees its sources for a re-claim.
10. **Claim window.** A source whose date is more than `min(return_policies.claim_window_days, schemes.claim_window_days)` days before `period_to` is inserted with `status = 'rejected'` and `detail.reason = 'out_of_window'` — visible loss, never a silent drop.
11. **Damage/expiry valuation.** Value at `return_policies.damage_value_basis` / `expiry_value_basis`: `ptd` → `tenant_product_costs.ptd_paise`, `landed_cost` → `landed_cost_paise`, and when `per_piece_cost numeric(14,4)` is set use it and round half-up to paise at the line total (docs/17 A4). MRP basis only when explicitly configured. `amount_paise = qty_pcs × rate_paise` computed with `multiply()`.
12. **Policy gates the build.** No damage lines when `damage_claimable = false`, no expiry lines when `expiry_claimable = false` (Alan's / Masti Oye take no returns) — refuse with a clear 409 naming the brand rather than producing an empty claim.
13. **Cancelled invoices contribute nothing**, and a credit note against an invoice reduces the claimable scheme quantity: claimable qty per invoice line = `invoice_lines.qty_pcs − Σ credit_note_lines.qty_pcs`, and the scheme amount is scaled by that ratio with `allocate()` so the paise still sum exactly.
14. **Free-goods scheme lines** are claimed at cost, not at selling rate: `qty_pcs = AppliedRule.freeQty`, `basis = 'ptd'`, `rate_paise` from `tenant_product_costs`. Percentage / flat / `net_scheme_amount` rewards are claimed at `AppliedRule.amountPaise` exactly as given, `basis = 'scheme_amount'`.
15. **Journals balance to zero to the paisa**, carry a deterministic `idempotency_key` (`claim:accrue:<id>`, `claim:settle:<settlementId>`, `claim:reject:<id>`, `claim:writeoff:<id>`), and reversals are new entries with `reversed_by_entry_id` set on the original — `journal_lines` is append-only by trigger, so a delete or update will raise.
16. **`settled_paise + written_off_paise ≤ claimed_paise`** always (service check → 409, DB check as the guarantee). A settlement never pushes a line's `settled_paise` above its `amount_paise`; the remainder spreads to the next line with `allocate()`.
17. **Never touch another module's tables.** Required service methods (add them to the owning module if absent): `BillingService.invoiceLinesForPeriod({ brandId, from, to, limit, cursor })`, `BillingService.creditNoteLinesForPeriod(...)`, `PricingService.schemesByIds(ids)`, `InventoryService.ledgerRowsByReason({ reasons, locationKind: 'damaged', from, to })`, `ProcurementService.openDiscrepancies({ supplierId, from, to })` + `markDiscrepanciesClaimed(ids, claimId)`, `TenantCatalogService.returnPolicy(brandId)` / `upsertReturnPolicy(input)` / `costsForVariants(variantIds)`, `ReceivablesService.postEntry({ entryDate, refType, refId, narration, idempotencyKey, lines: [{ accountCode, amountPaise, partyType, partyId, memo }] })` + `reverseEntry(entryId, idempotencyKey)`, `IntegrationsService.enqueueExport({ kind, params, requestedBy })`, `DocintService.get(documentId)`.
18. **Bounded work.** Lists cap at 500 (`claims.list` 200, statements 50); the build caps at 2,000 lines and reports `truncated`; `claims.periods.list` caps at 500 rows; `claims.reconcile.suggest` searches at most 20 claims and subsets of at most 3. Nothing in this module scans a tenant's whole history.
19. **Claims carry no GST.** They are commercial claims, not tax documents; `claim_lines` has no tax columns and the journal touches only the receivable and the expense account. A brand credit note that carries GST is booked in AP by the accountant; only its taxable base matches the claim.
20. **Audit.** `submit`, `reject`, `writeOff`, `settlements.record`, `policies.upsert`, `lines.add`, `lines.remove` each write an `audit_log` row (`entity_type = 'claim'`, before/after) — these are the money-moving actions.
21. **What must never happen:** a salesperson, warehouse, delivery or retailer actor reading any claims row (it reveals purchase cost and which schemes the brand funds — the Vyapar failure that made reps quit); a claims table reaching a device through PowerSync or `SyncRegistry`; a `brand_dms` claim producing a journal entry; a scheme line claimed twice; a number allocated to a draft; `status` assigned without the machine.

## 5. DB-backed spec list

`backend/libs/core/src/modules/claims/claims.spec.ts`, `describeDb` + `bootTestApp([TenantCatalogModule, PricingModule, BillingModule, ProcurementModule, InventoryModule, ReceivablesModule, ClaimsModule])`, fixtures created directly with `@dos/db` under a unique `run = uuidv7().slice(-8)` suffix, `bootstrapTenant()` for the chart of accounts and the CLAIM series.

1. `open → build → submit happy path` → claim moves `draft → submitted`, `claim_no` is `CLM-0001`, `claimed_paise = Σ line amounts`, one balanced journal entry exists with a `SCHEME_RECEIVABLE` debit for the supplier party.
2. `build is re-runnable` → a second `build` with a fresh idempotency key adds zero lines, `claimed_paise` unchanged, `skipped` equals the first run's `added`.
3. `build selects only company-funded, dos-channel, non-cash-discount schemes` → a distributor-funded scheme line, a `brand_dms` scheme line and a `cash_discount_pct` scheme line seeded on the same invoice are all absent from `claim_lines`.
4. `a source is claimable once` → a second claim over an overlapping period skips the invoice line already claimed; after `claims.reject` on the first claim, the rebuild of the second picks it up.
5. `idempotent submit` → same `idempotencyKey` twice returns the identical body and exactly one `numbering_series` bump; the same key with a changed payload returns 409.
6. `state machine guards` → `submit` on a `submitted` claim → 409; `acknowledge` on a `draft` → 409; `writeOff` on a `settled` → 409.
7. `brand_dms claim posts no journal` → submit allocates `claim_no` and sets `submitted_at`, `accrued_at IS NULL`, and `journal_entries` has no row with `ref_id = claim.id`.
8. `partial then full settlement` → `partially_settled` then `settled`; `settled_paise` sums exactly; two journal entries, each balancing to zero; the per-line `settled_paise` from `allocate()` sums to the settlement amount with no paisa lost.
9. `over-settlement refused` → a settlement exceeding `claimed_paise − settled_paise` → 409 and no `claim_settlements` row (DB check is the backstop).
10. `reject reverses the accrual` → a compensating entry exists, the accrual's `reversed_by_entry_id` points at it, no journal row was deleted, and all non-settled lines are `rejected`.
11. `write-off closes the remainder` → `settled_paise + written_off_paise = claimed_paise`, status `written_off`, a `DAMAGES` debit for a damage-kind claim and `SCHEME_EXPENSE` for a scheme-kind claim.
12. `ageing buckets` → a claim submitted 45 days ago lands in `bucket31to60Paise`, a draft in `notSubmittedPaise`, a `brand_dms` claim appears in `groups` but not in `totals.outstandingPaise`.
13. `damage valuation uses the policy basis` → lines carry `basis = 'ptd'` and `rate_paise = tenant_product_costs.ptd_paise`, never `mrp_paise`; a brand with `damage_claimable = false` makes `build` return 409 naming the brand.
14. `out-of-window sources are recorded, not dropped` → a credit note older than `claim_window_days` produces a `rejected` line with `detail.reason = 'out_of_window'` and is excluded from `claimed_paise`.
15. `statement generation never streams bytes` → `claims.statements.generate` creates one `export_jobs` row (`kind = 'claim_sheet'`) and one `claim_statements` row with `object_key IS NULL`, `ready = false`.
16. `RLS — salesperson` → `GET /claims` → 403, and `withTenant(db, { actorRole: 'salesperson' }, tx => tx.select().from(claims))` returns 0 rows even though the rows exist for that tenant.
17. `RLS — warehouse` → `GET /claims/ageing` → 403, and a `withTenant` select on `claim_lines` as `warehouse` returns 0 rows (this is the purchase-cost leak vector: `claim_lines.rate_paise`).
18. `RLS — delivery` → `POST /claims/{id}/settlements` → 403, and a direct insert into `claim_settlements` as `delivery` raises a row-level-security violation.
19. `RLS — retailer` → `GET /claims/{id}` → 403 and a `withTenant` select on `claims` as `retailer` returns 0 rows — a shop must never learn what the brand funds.
20. `role split inside the back office` → accountant can `settlements.record` and `writeOff` but gets 403 on `policies.upsert`; manager can `build`/`submit` but gets 403 on `writeOff`; owner can do all three.
21. `tenant isolation` → a claim of tenant B is invisible to tenant A's owner through both the API (404) and a direct `withTenant` select (0 rows).
22. `no context` → every procedure without the actor headers → 401.
23. Extend `backend/libs/database/src/rls.test.ts`: `claim_settlements` joins the enumeration asserting FORCE RLS + at least one policy on every `tenant_id` table, and the cost-leak test gains "`claim_lines` unreadable by salesperson/warehouse/delivery/retailer".

## 7. Worker jobs / outbox events

**Outbox events** (`aggregate_type = 'claim'`, inserted in the same transaction as the state change): `ClaimSubmitted` `{ claimId, claimNo, supplierId, brandId, kind, claimChannel, claimedPaise, dueDate }`; `ClaimSettled` `{ claimId, settlementId, amountPaise, mode, settledPaise, status }`; `ClaimRejected` `{ claimId, reason }`; `ClaimWrittenOff` `{ claimId, writtenOffPaise }`; `ClaimStatementRequested` `{ claimId, statementId, format, exportJobId }`.

**pg-boss consumers in `backend/worker`:**

- `claims.statement.render` — triggered by the relayed `ClaimStatementRequested` (and by an `export_jobs` row of `kind = 'claim_sheet'`). Runs as `app_worker`, loads the claim + lines, renders the brand column set (`reliance_xlsx`, `guru_kripa_xlsx`, `mom_foods_email`, `field_assist`, fallback `generic_xlsx`) to object storage, then fills `claim_statements.object_key` / `row_count` / `generated_at` and closes the `export_jobs` row. Bounded concurrency; retries are safe (same object key).
- `claims.period.rollover` — daily cron 02:00 IST. For every tenant × brand whose `claim_cutoff_day` matched yesterday, opens the next period's draft claim per claimable kind and runs the same build the endpoint runs, leaving it in `draft` for the back office. Per-tenant fairness: one job per tenant, keyed by `tenant_id`.
- `claims.overdue.sweep` — weekly cron. Claims in `submitted | acknowledged | partially_settled` with `due_date < today` emit `ClaimOverdue` `{ claimId, claimNo, supplierId, outstandingPaise, daysOverdue }` for the notifications module (owner WhatsApp/push) once per claim per week.
- No retention job: claims are permanent financial records.

## 8. Founder questions this brief assumed an answer for

These map to docs/17 §C item 7 ("return/damage policy and claim sheet format per brand") and item 8 ("does the CA already key Too Yumm invoices into Tally"), both still open.

1. **Claim cadence** — assumed monthly per brand with a cut-off on the 1st for the previous calendar month, overridable per brand via `claim_period_kind` / `claim_cutoff_day`; Balaji/Guru Kripa seeded as fortnightly because super-stockists settle faster.
2. **Settlement days** — assumed 30 (Reliance, MOM Foods), 21 (Guru Kripa), 15 (Guiltfree/FieldAssist); drives `due_date` and the ageing buckets only.
3. **Claim sheet formats** — assumed the four seeded strings (`reliance_xlsx`, `guru_kripa_xlsx`, `mom_foods_email`, `field_assist`) are column sets implemented as `backend/libs/core/src/modules/claims/formats/*.ts`, with a `generic_xlsx` fallback. Real column headers must come from Tarsun's actual claim sheets before the pilot.
4. **Damage/expiry valuation basis** — assumed PTD (`ptd_paise`), falling back to `landed_cost_paise`, never MRP. Some brands reimburse at PTR; the per-brand `damage_value_basis` column exists precisely so this can be corrected without a migration.
5. **Free-goods claim value** — assumed free pieces are claimed at cost (PTD), not at the selling rate; percentage rewards are claimed at the exact `AppliedRule.amountPaise` given away.
6. **Cash discount is not claimable** — assumed, because ADR 0004 realises it at receipt; if a brand actually reimburses the cash discount, rule 8 of §4 is the single place to change.
7. **`brand_dms` claims never hit our journal** — assumed (they are settled inside FieldAssist against the brand's own ledger). If the CA books those credit notes in Tally, this becomes a double count and the assumption must flip to "accrue, and mark the Tally export source `brand_dms`".
8. **Claims carry no GST** — assumed commercial claims; a brand credit note with GST is booked in AP by the accountant and only its taxable base matches the claim. If the brands issue GST credit notes that we must mirror, `claim_lines` needs tax columns (an expand-only follow-up).
9. **`written_off` added to `claim_status`** — assumed the business wants to close unrecoverable claims rather than leave them open forever; write-off is owner/accountant only.
10. **Warehouse role gets nothing from this module** — assumed: the crew records damage as an inventory movement, and the money view of that damage stays in the back office.
