## Summary for the founder

When a shop is closed or refuses the goods, the bill rides back on the van. Today the godown can never load it again properly: it drops off the loading list for good, and the next van quietly carries it off with no count, no challan and no e-way bill check.
Fix: the bill waits on its van until the vehicle is checked in. Then it comes back on the planning board and the loading list and goes out on a fresh load sheet for its next trip, with the manager's approval, the count, a new challan and the e-way bill check on the whole load.
One question for you: may a van still leave with a bill nobody counted out at the godown (recommended: no, the van waits for its load sheet).

## Root cause

Confirmed on main 10c2464; every line matches the skeptic's check.

1. A CONFIRMED SHEET HOLDS ITS BILLS FOR EVER. `load-sheets.service.ts:795-807` `onALiveSheet` counts every sheet with `status <> 'cancelled'`, and `create` refuses on it at :185-189. The same rule, `onLiveLoadSheet` (`packing.service.ts:388-391`), filters `packs.list?status=awaiting_load` at :186-188, so W7 hides the bill. Nothing releases an order from a confirmed sheet. `failStopInTx` (`trips.service.ts:1000-1069`) is shared by `stops.fail`, the `/sync/upload` `trip_stops` handler (`delivery.sync.ts:100-118` → `failStopFromInput` :889) and `trips.return` (:551-554). It and a refused-at-door delivery (`deliveries.service.ts:166-176`) only move the order `dispatched → packed`, and `cancel` refuses a confirmed sheet (:498-501). The only exit is `depart`'s `dispatchIfPacked` (:507-508, :982-997), which dispatches any packed bill on the trip with no sheet, count, challan or e-way bill check, reason `'trip depart'`.
2. THE BOARD AND THE GODOWN DISAGREE ABOUT WHERE THE BILL IS. `plannedOnOpenTrips` (`delivery.internals.ts:373-409`) claims a bill only through an outcome-null delivery row. So the moment the stop fails, with the van still out, the board offers the bill (`trips.service.ts:388-394`) and any trip may take it (`insertStop` :1124-1128), while the godown's list hides it. Neither side matches the physical truth: the goods ride back on the van and reach the dock only at check-in.
3. THE SEED ALREADY MODELS THE RIGHT THING. `seed-demo/warehouse.ts:246-248` puts an order that failed at the door on both days' sheets, and `sales.ts:2847` records "goods back on the dock". The 409 pinned in `warehouse.spec.ts:2002-2049` is list/create parity, not a product decision (DOS-133 verdict (d)).

## Design

Build order below; paths from /Users/prajwalchavan/Desktop/Distribution OS. Test-first: every step names its test. No migration, permission, RLS or table change. There is one additive contract field. `deliveries.service.ts` and `delivery.sync.ts` are NOT edited: the online, offline and refused-at-door paths all end in the same in-transaction methods, and the rules below fix them there.

THE SHAPE (three rules, each testable alone; one predicate behind rules A and B):
- Rule A, the sheet: only a DRAFT sheet holds a bill. A confirmed sheet is the record of one load-out and never a claim. `confirm` dispatches every order on the sheet in the same transaction (:428-438), so a `packed` order listed on a confirmed sheet can only be one that came back.
- Rule B, the road: a bill that came back undelivered is HELD on its trip from the failed stop until `trips.return` (trip `closing`). While it is held, every server door refuses it and every list leaves it out: planning (`trips.create`, `stops.add`, the same trip included), the board (listed under `held` with its trip number), W7's `awaiting_load` and `loadSheets.create`. Settlement plays no part (the bill's cartons left the ledger at pack, DOS-039). ONE delivery-side predicate answers "held". Warehouse asks it through a lookup that delivery registers at start-up, the DOS-132 `registerTripSettled` pattern, so warehouse never names `deliveries` or `trips`.
- Rule C, the gate: a bill leaves the godown only through a confirmed load sheet. `depart` refuses a trip that carries a bill still `packed` (409 `bill_not_loaded`), and `dispatchIfPacked` is deleted. This is the founder question; Rules A and B stand under either answer.

STEP 1 — delivery's predicate and its registration (`delivery.internals.ts`, `delivery.module.ts`).
1a. `plannedOnOpenTrips` → `ridingTrips(tx, invoiceIds): Promise<Map<string, RidingTrip>>` with `export interface RidingTrip { tripId: string; tripNo: string | null; how: 'planned' | 'returned_on_road' }`. Predicate: `(d.outcome is null and t.state not in ('settled','settled_with_variance','cancelled')) or (d.outcome = 'failed' and t.state = 'active')`. It selects `trips.trip_no` through the existing join, ordered `(d.outcome is null) desc` so a planned row wins, and first row per invoice wins. It still runs under `asSystemRole`, ids only, behind a literal tenant fence, on `deliveries_invoice_idx`. JSDoc: the two ways a bill rides a trip; `closing` frees a failed bill because the van is at the dock.
1b. `export async function returnedOnTheRoad(tx, invoiceIds): Promise<Map<string, { tripId: string; tripNo: string | null }>>` = `ridingTrips` narrowed to `how === 'returned_on_road'`.
1c. `DeliveryModule` constructor gains `LoadSheetsService` (already exported by warehouse/index.ts). In `onModuleInit`, right after `registerTripSettled` and before the sync early return, call `this.loadSheets.registerRoadHold(returnedOnTheRoad)`. Update the class comment: delivery also tells the godown which bills are still on the road (DOS-172).

## Binding amendments

- (a) Only a draft sheet holds a bill; `onADraftSheet` and `onDraftLoadSheet` are the same predicate (`status = 'draft'`), tied by the DOS-133 spec. A confirmed sheet and its challan are never edited, cancelled or annotated.
- (b) Held = a `failed` delivery row of an `active` trip, answered by `ridingTrips` alone. `insertStop`, `planning`, `loadSheets.create` and `packs.list?status=awaiting_load` all ask it. Warehouse asks through `registerRoadHold`, never by reading delivery's tables. The hold starts at the failed stop (online, offline or refused at the door) and ends at `trips.return`. A stop left open at return is failed and freed in the same transaction.
- (c) Every service that mounts `WarehouseModule` mounts `DeliveryModule` (owner, manager, warehouse, delivery; `service/definitions.ts`). A static test pins this, because the empty default lookup would otherwise offer a held bill silently.
- (d) `depart` dispatches nothing. Under answer A it refuses `bill_not_loaded` after the draft check and before consent. A refusal writes no audit row and emits no event, and `'trip depart'` is never written again. Answer B: 4c.
- (e) `TripPlanningOutput.held` is additive; `bills` keeps its meaning (plannable now). There is no other shape, permission, policy or migration change. READMEs are regenerated on the merged tree, never edited.
- (f) The e-way bill gate is unchanged in code: `create` values every order's bill plus van stock, and `confirm` refuses `ewb_required` (:355-359).
- (g) The road rule has exactly one server home. No screen carries a copy: W7 shows what `awaiting_load` returns, and W10/M7 only display `held`.
- (h) Any spec, the smoke harness included, that departs a trip carrying a bill loads it out through a confirmed sheet first (5a–5d). Only DOS-133's (d) row keeps a direct `applyFulfilmentEvent` dispatch, as a legacy order.
- (i) Files outside the list are not edited. docs/22, docs/23 and the stale W7 comment go to the main session and group 9.
