# DOS-172 — architect design (Fable, 2026-09-14)

Run `wf_4254a4ee-d2f`. P1, confirmed on the API by probe and skeptic (QA/evidence/batch2/suspects/S-03/), NOT yet approved by the founder. S-82 is the same defect and is merged into this record. Everything below is decided by the architect except the one founder question at the end.

## Summary for the founder

When a shop is closed or refuses the goods, the bill comes back on the van, and today the godown can never load it again the proper way: the system keeps it marked "already loaded" for ever, and the next trip quietly carries it off with no count, no challan and no e-way bill check.
Fix: a bill counts as loaded only while its load sheet is still being built; once the van has checked in, the returned bill is offered again like any other packed bill and goes on a fresh load sheet for its next trip, with the manager's approval, the blind count, a new challan and the e-way bill check on the whole load, and until the van is back the planning board shows it as "out on TRIP-x".
One question for you: may a trip still leave with a bill nobody counted out at the godown (recommended: no, the trip waits for the load sheet).

## Root cause

Confirmed by reading current main (199952b and after); every line matches the skeptic's check.

1. A CONFIRMED SHEET HOLDS ITS BILLS FOR EVER. `load-sheets.service.ts:795-807` `onALiveSheet` counts every sheet with `status <> 'cancelled'`; `create` refuses on it at :185-189. The same rule is `onLiveLoadSheet` in `packing.service.ts:388-391`, which `packs.list?status=awaiting_load` filters with (:186-188), so W7's "Packed orders" hides the bill. Nothing releases an order from a confirmed sheet: `failStopInTx` (`trips.service.ts:1000-1069`, shared by `stops.fail`, the `/sync/upload` `trip_stops` handler `delivery.sync.ts:100-118` and `trips.return` :551-554) and a refused-at-door delivery (`deliveries.service.ts:166-176`) only move the order `dispatched → packed`; `cancel` refuses a confirmed sheet (:498-501). The only exit is `depart`'s `dispatchIfPacked` (:507-508, :983-997), which dispatches any packed bill on the trip with no sheet, count, challan or e-way bill check, reason `'trip depart'`.
2. THE BOARD AND THE GODOWN DISAGREE ON WHERE THE BILL IS. `plannedOnOpenTrips` (`delivery.internals.ts:385-409`) claims a bill only through an outcome-null delivery row, so the moment the stop fails — van still out — the board offers the bill (`trips.service.ts:388-394`) and any trip may take it (`insertStop` :1124-1128), while the godown's list hides it. Neither side knows the physical truth of docs/22 §4 D5: the goods ride back on the van and are on the dock only after check-in.
3. THE SEED ALREADY MODELS THE RIGHT THING. `seed-demo/warehouse.ts:246-248` puts an order that failed at the door on both days' sheets ("went out again two days later"), and `sales.ts:2847` records "goods back on the dock" — the product intent is a second load-out on a second sheet. The 409 pinned in `warehouse.spec.ts:2002-2049` is list/create parity, not a product decision (DOS-133 verdict (d) named this change).

## Design

Build order below; paths from /Users/prajwalchavan/Desktop/Distribution OS. Test-first: every step names its test (see `Tests`). No migration, no permission change, no RLS change, no new table. One additive contract field. `deliveries.service.ts` and `delivery.sync.ts` are NOT edited: the online, offline and refused-at-door paths all end in the same in-transaction methods and are fixed by the rules below.

THE SHAPE OF THE FIX (three rules, each testable alone):
- Rule A, the sheet: only a DRAFT sheet holds a bill. A confirmed sheet is the record of one load-out (its challan declared what left that day) and never a claim; a cancelled sheet never was one. `loadSheets.confirm` dispatches every order on the sheet in the same transaction (:428-438), so a `packed` order listed on a confirmed sheet can only be one that came back.
- Rule B, the road: a bill that came back undelivered rides its trip until the van checks in. From the failed stop to `trips.return` the bill is HELD on that trip — the board says so, no other trip (and no new stop on the same trip) may take it, W7 does not offer it. After `return` (trip `closing`) it is free; settlement plays no part (settlement counts van stock, and the bill's cartons are off the ledger since pack, QA DOS-039).
- Rule C, the gate: a bill leaves the godown only through a confirmed load sheet. `depart` refuses a trip carrying a bill still `packed` (409 `bill_not_loaded`); `dispatchIfPacked` and the `'trip depart'` dispatch are deleted. This is the founder question; the rest of the design stands under either answer.

## Binding amendments

- (a) Only a draft sheet holds a bill; `onADraftSheet` and `onDraftLoadSheet` are the same predicate (`status = 'draft'`) and the warehouse.spec DOS-133 test stays their tie. A confirmed sheet and its challan are never edited, cancelled or annotated by this fix.
- (b) A bill that came back undelivered is held on its trip from the failed stop (online, offline or refused at the door) until `trips.return`; settlement is not a step. The hold is a delivery-side fact (`plannedOnOpenTrips`), told to the godown through the board's `held` list, never by warehouse reading `deliveries`.
- (c) `insertStop` refuses a held bill on every trip, the same one included, with `bill_on_road`; the board never offers a bill the guard would refuse (the DOS-131 principle stands) and lists the held ones with their trip number instead.
- (d) `depart` dispatches nothing. Under answer A it refuses a `packed` bill with `bill_not_loaded` after the draft-sheet check and before the consent check; a refusal writes no audit row and emits no event. Under answer B the quiet dispatch is kept but audited (3c). The order's dispatch transition from a sheet keeps reason `null`; `'trip depart'` is never written again under A.
- (e) `TripPlanningOutput.held` is additive; `bills` keeps its meaning (plannable now). No other contract, permission, policy or migration changes. READMEs regenerated, never edited.
- (f) The e-way bill gate is unchanged in code: `create` values the sheet at Σ invoice totals of the orders it lists plus van stock and `confirm` refuses `ewb_required` without a number (:355-359); Rule C is what makes it cover every bill that leaves.
- (g) W7's loadable rows come from `notThisTripsLoad(board)` (bills ∪ held); no screen gains a second copy of the road rule, and no screen imports anything but `@dos/ui`, `@dos/api-client`, `@dos/contracts`.
- (h) Every existing spec that departs a trip with packed bills gets a `loadOut(tripId, orderIds)` helper (create → manager approve → confirm through the endpoints); no spec dispatches through `applyFulfilmentEvent` to dodge the gate except DOS-133's (d) row, which models a legacy dispatched-with-no-sheet order and is kept.
- (i) Files outside the list are not edited; docs/22 and docs/23 sentences and the QA e2e script go to the main session and the verifier.

## Founder question

**May a trip leave the godown with a bill that nobody counted out on a load sheet?** Today the van can: the bill is marked dispatched quietly at departure and no challan or e-way bill covers it; every bill that comes back undelivered takes this road on its second trip.

Recommended: **A — No.** The trip is refused until the godown builds and confirms the load sheet for those bills; the driver's screen says "2 bills on TRIP-0012 have not been counted out at the godown: SO-0845, SO-0850; build and confirm the load sheet first". Nothing leaves without the manager's approval, the blind count, a challan and the e-way bill check, which is what you decided on 2026-09-05 and in DOS-043; a ₹1 lakh load can no longer go out without its e-way bill number.

- Alternative B — Yes, keep the quiet departure, but write on the trip and the bill that it left without a load sheet so the desk can see it. Cheaper by half a day, and nothing blocks a crew; but the count, the challan and the e-way bill stay optional on every bill that ever comes back, and the vehicle can still be detained.

Rows already wrong in the databases: none need a correcting entry. The four seeded bills (tarsun SO-0845 and SO-0850 on DC-0080, kalyan-agencies SO-0169 on DC-0037, sai-distributors SO-0435 on DC-0050 — in dos_qa, in the founder's own dos if it carries the realistic seed, and in every fresh seed) become plannable and loadable by the new read-time rules with no row changed; no stock or journal row was written wrong (the goods left as `sale` at pack, the invoice stays open, the failed stop is recorded). Bills that already left through `'trip depart'` in QA walks keep that transition row; a challan cannot be issued after the fact and nothing in stock or money is affected. The verifier runs one read-only query listing them (`order_state_transitions where event = 'dispatch' and reason = 'trip depart'`) into the evidence folder for the record.
