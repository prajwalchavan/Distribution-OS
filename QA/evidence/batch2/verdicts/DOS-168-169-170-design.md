# DOS-168 · DOS-169 · DOS-170 — architect design (Fable, 2026-09-14)

Run `wf_4254a4ee-d2f`. One design for the three money findings that share `receivables.service.ts`, `settlement.service.ts` and `receivables.sync.ts`. NOT yet approved by the founder; nothing is built before the founder answers the two questions below.

## Summary for the founder

Three money faults, all confirmed on the real API: two people banking the same receipt at the same moment bank it twice; a payment a driver took at a door with no signal is missed when the trip's cash is counted at day-end, so an honest driver looks "over" and a driver who keeps that cash passes green; and undoing a doorstep cash receipt after day-end takes the money out of the van's account a second time instead of the office till.
Fix: every receipt is locked while it moves (banked, undone, or counted at day-end), so two moves can never both win; day-end counts the trip's receipts themselves, whichever way they arrived, net of any undone ones; and an undo takes the money from wherever it actually is: the van before day-end, the office till after it, the bank once banked.
Two questions for you: what the phone should do with a doorstep receipt that reaches the office only after that trip was already settled (recommended: refuse it and tell the driver to hand the cash to the cashier), and whether any wrong figures found in your own database should be corrected by one extra balancing entry each, with your say per trip.

## Root cause

Confirmed by reading current main (/Users/prajwalchavan/Desktop/Distribution OS) against the probes in QA/evidence/batch2/suspects/S-75b, S-76, S-75a:

Fully offline (stops + delivery + receipt in one batch) the settlement has no CASH_VAN line at all. Same for UPI and cheque figures.
3. DOS-170. `undoReceipt` (:1921-1923) picks the source as `depositedAt ? 'BANK' : receiptAccountCode(mode, tripId)` — CASH_VAN for any trip cash, whether or not the trip settled. The settlement already moved that cash CASH_VAN → CASH (:235-239); `this.tripSettled` (the DOS-132 predicate, :318-330) is consulted by `getReceipt` (:791) and `depositReceipts` (:902-907) but not here; the reversal half was left out of DOS-132 on purpose (verdicts/DOS-132.md, Decisions) and never filed until S-75.
4. The two untested neighbours, settled by code reading: (a) a trip cash receipt REVERSED BEFORE settlement stays in `collections` (receivables cannot touch that table), so today the settlement counts it (expected cash too high: an honest crew shows short) and credits CASH_VAN for it a second time (the reversal already did) → CASH_VAN ends −X. (b) `settled_with_variance` is in `TRIP_CASH_HANDED_OVER` (delivery.internals.ts:138-141), so DOS-170 applies to it exactly as to `settled`; test T9's control covers it.
5. There is no single definition of where a receipt's money is. Deposit, undo and settlement each compute it from different rows (status, `depositedAt`, trip state, `collections`) under no common lock, so two of them can disagree inside one second.

## Design

Everything below is in build order. Paths from /Users/prajwalchavan/Desktop/Distribution OS. Test-first: every step names its tests (see Tests). No contract shape, permission, migration or sync-protocol change: the contract JSDoc of `SettlementPreviewOutput` and `collectionsCount` is reworded only (`pnpm docs:readme` after; expect no README diff), so `pnpm smoke` and the seven apps' READMEs stand.

ONE DEFINITION (the rule every path implements). A receipt's money is in exactly one place, decided by its own row plus the trip's state:
- WITH THE CREW: `mode = cash`, `trip_id` set, status `collected`, trip NOT in `TRIP_CASH_HANDED_OVER`. Account CASH_VAN. Only a settlement moves it (Cr CASH_VAN / Dr CASH).
- IN THE OFFICE: status `collected`, `deposited_at` null, and either no `trip_id` or the trip settled (`settled` / `settled_with_variance`). Account CASH (cash), CHEQUES (cheque), UPI. A deposit moves cash and cheques to BANK; an undo credits the office account.
- IN THE BANK: `deposited_at` set. An undo or bounce credits BANK.
- GONE: status `cancelled` or `bounced`. Counted nowhere, movable by nothing.
Cheques and UPI are never "with the crew" for money purposes (they never enter expected cash); a bounced trip cheque still counts in the informational cheque figure (the paper was handed over).

ONE LOCK DISCIPLINE (why the paths cannot disagree). The receipt row `FOR UPDATE` is the lock a money move holds; the trip's state is read only while holding it; nobody takes a trip lock after a receipt lock; a per-trip transaction advisory lock serialises "count the trip's receipts" against "add a receipt to the trip". Orders: settle = trip row FOR UPDATE → advisory(trip) → the trip's receipts FOR UPDATE by id; online collection / van sale = trip row FOR UPDATE → advisory(trip) → insert; offline receipt = advisory(trip) → settled check → insert; deposit = its receipts FOR UPDATE by id → settled check; undo = its one receipt FOR UPDATE → settled check. No path waits for a lock while holding one that a path ahead of it in this order wants, so there is no cycle. READ COMMITTED re-reads the row after the lock is granted, so the loser always sees the winner's committed status or trip state.

STEP 1 — `ReceivablesService` (backend/libs/core/src/modules/receivables/receivables.service.ts).
1a. `depositReceipts`: the batch read (:876-879) becomes `.orderBy(asc(receipts.id)).for('update')`; keep the status, mode and DOS-132 checks as they are; the UPDATE (:933-942) gains `eq(receipts.status, 'collected')` and `.returning({ id: receipts.id })`, and a returned count ≠ `input.receiptIds.length` throws `ORPCError('CONFLICT', { message: 'a receipt in this batch was banked or undone by someone else a moment ago; reload the register', data: { code: 'receipt_moved' } })` — it rolls the journal and events back with it. The loser of a race therefore answers the existing 409 "receipt X is deposited, not collected" (its locked re-read shows the winner's status); the guarded UPDATE is the second net.
1b. `undoReceipt`: the original's read (:1862-1866) becomes `.for('update')`. The source account (:1921-1923) becomes: `depositedAt` → BANK; else `mode === 'cash' && tripId !== null && await this.isTripSettled(tx, tripId, tenantId)` → CASH; else `receiptAccountCode(mode, tripId)`. New private `isTripSettled(tx, tripId, tenantId): Promise<boolean>` = the `getReceipt` :791-794 statement, shared by both callers (getReceipt keeps its shape). The mirror row keeps `tripId` (the list's `withCrew` and the register still name the trip).
1c. `recordReceipt` (:610): after the replay check (:616-617) and before drawing the number, when `input.tripId` is set: `await tx.execute(sql\`select pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext(${'trip-money:' + tenantId + ':' + tripId}))\`)`, then `if (await this.isTripSettled(tx, tripId, tenantId)) throw new ORPCError('CONFLICT', { message: \`trip ${tripId} has already settled; hand this money to the cashier and record it at the office, not on the trip\`, data: { code: 'trip_settled', tripId } })`. With no predicate registered (a service without delivery, the receivables spec) `isTripSettled` is `false` as today — no lock is taken, nothing is refused. Online collections and van sales already hold the trip row FOR UPDATE, so the advisory lock only adds the serialisation against a settlement in flight. This is FOUNDER QUESTION 1's default A.
1d. New public `tripMoney(tx, tripId, { lock }: { lock: boolean }): Promise<{ cashPaise; upiPaise; chequePaise; count }>`: when `lock`, first the advisory lock of 1c, then `select id from receipts where tenant_id = $t and trip_id = $trip order by id for update`; then one aggregate over receipts `where tenant_id and trip_id and reverses_receipt_id is null and status <> 'cancelled'` grouped by mode (`FOR UPDATE` cannot sit on an aggregate, hence two statements). A reversed original is `cancelled` and its mirror carries `reverses_receipt_id`, so both drop out; a bounced cheque original stays (`bounced`). `count` = rows counted, all modes. Export nothing new from `index.ts` (a method on the already-exported service).
1e. `receivables.sync.ts` `applyReceiptSync`: wrap the `recordReceipt` call; an `ORPCError` whose `data.code === 'trip_settled'` is rethrown as `SyncRejection('trip_settled', error.message)` so the tray shows the code, not `conflict`. Everything else unchanged.

STEP 2 — `SettlementService` (backend/libs/core/src/modules/delivery/settlement.service.ts).
2a. `figures(tx, trip, { lock })`: cash, UPI and cheque come from `this.receivables.tripMoney(tx, trip.id, { lock })`; `collectionsCount` = its `count`; expenses, policy and van stock as today. `collections` is no longer read here (the import goes if unused). `cockpit`/`preview` pass `lock: false`; `plan` inside the idempotent `settle` transaction passes `lock: true` (after `lockTrip`, so the order is trip → advisory → receipts); the pre-check `withTenant` at :117-126 stays lock-free (it only decides whether to file the approval; the locked plan decides the money).
2b. Settled trips read their settled figures: in `cockpit`, when `existing` (a `trip_settlements` row) is present, `cashCollectedPaise = existing.expectedCashPaise − trip.openingCashPaise + existing.expensesPaise`, `upiCollectedPaise = existing.upiCollectedPaise`, `expensesPaise` and `expectedCashPaise` from the row; `chequeCollectedPaise` and `collectionsCount` stay live. So a reversal after day-end (which now credits CASH) never re-writes what the cockpit says was handed over.
2c. The journal lines (:219-240) are untouched: with `plan.cashCollectedPaise` now the receipts' net, Cr CASH_VAN equals the Dr CASH_VAN those receipts posted, by construction, and CASH_SHORT carries only real variance.

STEP 3 — delivery app check-in gate (frontend/delivery-app/app/day.tsx, src/strings.ts, new src/lib/check-in.ts).

## Binding amendments

- (a) The receipt row `FOR UPDATE` is the only lock a money move holds on receipts, and the trip's state is read only under it; `depositReceipts`, `undoReceipt` and `tripMoney({ lock: true })` lock in ascending `receipts.id`; no path takes a trip lock after a receipt lock. Any future receipt writer follows the same order (say so in the `undoReceipt` doc comment).
- (b) The per-trip advisory lock is `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext('trip-money:<tenantId>:<tripId>'))`, transaction-scoped, taken by `recordReceipt` for a trip receipt and by `tripMoney({ lock: true })`, never held across a request and never taken for an office receipt. A hash collision only over-serialises, never mis-locks.
- (c) The guarded UPDATE in `depositReceipts` refuses the WHOLE batch (`receipt_moved`, 409) when any row moved; nothing is partially banked. The loser of a plain race sees the existing "is deposited, not collected" sentence because its locked read already shows the winner.
- (d) Settlement counts `receipts` with the trip's id, net of reversals, per mode; `collections` is stop-level detail and is never summed for money again. Cr CASH_VAN at settlement therefore equals Σ Dr CASH_VAN of the counted receipts; a test pins that identity (T5, T7).
- (e) An undo's source is decided by the ONE DEFINITION: BANK if deposited, CASH if trip cash whose trip settled (either settled state), else the receipt's landing account; read under the row lock so it cannot disagree with a settlement.
- (f) A receipt for a trip that has settled is refused at record time with `trip_settled` (409 online, a `trip_settled` sync rejection offline), after the replay check, so a re-sent op that already landed is still a replay. Pending FOUNDER QUESTION 1; if the founder picks B or C the refusal becomes the chosen posting and tests T6 and T6' swap their expectations.
- (g) A settled trip's cockpit reports the figures it settled with (2b); nothing recomputes cash or UPI for a closed trip. The trip's live `chequeCollectedPaise` stays live (it never entered the settlement).
- (h) Frontend: only `day.tsx`, delivery `strings.ts` and the new pure module; the button copy comes from strings; no other screen changes. The manager Day-end "cash over" hint goes to lean-manager-money (Conflicts), not here.
- (i) No contract shape, PERMISSIONS, migration or `SYNC_PULL_TABLES` change; `pnpm docs:readme:check` passes with no diff after the JSDoc rewording, and `pnpm smoke` stays 0 BROKEN (its deposit and receipt examples carry no `tripId`).
- (j) New specs go in NEW files (`receivables/money-locks.spec.ts`, `delivery/settlement-money.spec.ts`) so lean-owner-money-approvals' and lean-delivery-collect's additions to the existing specs merge without conflict; the existing DOS-132 tests must stay green untouched.

## Founder questions

**1. A driver's phone was dead all evening; the trip was settled at Day-end on the cash counted; next morning the phone sends a ₹672 cash receipt for that trip. What should happen?**

Recommended: A — refuse it. The phone's Needs-attention tray reads "Trip TRIP-0007 was settled before this receipt arrived. Hand ₹672 from Chavan Kirana to the cashier; the office records it." The cashier records an office receipt when the cash changes hands, the shop's bill is paid then, and nothing is ever posted against a closed trip. The check-in gate (Step 3) makes this rare: with signal at the godown the phone sends everything before the vehicle can be checked in.
- B — accept it as van cash: the books say the crew still holds ₹672 on a trip that is closed, with no screen to hand it over (a second settlement does not exist).
- C — accept it as office cash straight away: the books say the office has the money the moment the phone syncs, before anyone handed it over.

**2. If the read-only checks in Data repair find figures already wrong in your own `dos` database, may they be corrected with one extra balancing entry each, listed for your sign-off trip by trip?**

Recommended: A — yes, one appended entry per case, never an edit, with your say on each trip (whether the crew handed that cash over or still has it). B — no, rebuild `dos` from the seed (loses the receipts recorded there). dos_qa needs neither: it is rebuilt from the seed at the A.12 rebuild, and the probes ran on disposable copies.
