## Summary for the founder

Three money faults, all confirmed on the real API. If two people bank the same receipt at the same moment, it is banked twice. A payment a driver takes at a door with no signal is missed when the trip's cash is counted at day-end, so an honest driver looks "over" and a driver who keeps that cash passes green. Undoing a doorstep cash receipt after day-end takes the money out of the van's account a second time instead of the office till.
The fix has four parts. Every receipt is locked while it is banked, undone or counted at day-end, so two of those moves can never both win. Day-end counts the trip's own receipts, however they arrived, net of any that were undone. An undo takes the money from wherever it really is: the van before day-end, the office till after it, the bank once it is banked. The driver's phone will not check the vehicle in while its payments are still on the phone.
Two questions for you. First, what should happen to a doorstep receipt that reaches the office only after its trip was settled? (Recommended: refuse it and tell the driver to hand the cash to the cashier.) Second, should wrong figures found in your own database be corrected by one extra balancing entry each, with your say on each trip?

## Design

Build order, test-first; paths from /Users/prajwalchavan/Desktop/Distribution OS. No contract shape, PERMISSIONS, migration, `SYNC_PULL_TABLES` or README change.

ONE DEFINITION. A receipt's money is in exactly one place, decided by its own row plus its trip's state:
- WITH THE CREW: `mode = cash`, `trip_id` set, status `collected`, and the trip is not in `TRIP_CASH_HANDED_OVER`. Account CASH_VAN. Only a settlement moves it (Cr CASH_VAN / Dr CASH).
- IN THE OFFICE: status `collected`, `deposited_at` null, and either no `trip_id` or a trip in `settled` / `settled_with_variance`. Account CASH, CHEQUES or UPI. A deposit moves it to BANK; an undo credits the office account.
- IN THE BANK: `deposited_at` set. An undo or a bounce credits BANK.
- GONE: status `cancelled` or `bounced`. Counted nowhere and movable by nothing. Cheques and UPI never enter expected cash. A bounced trip cheque still counts in the informational cheque figure.

ONE LOCK ORDER. A money move holds the receipt row `FOR UPDATE`, and it reads the trip's state only while holding that lock. Nobody takes a trip lock after a receipt lock. A per-trip transaction advisory lock serialises "count the trip's receipts" against "add a receipt to the trip". The order for each path:
- settle: trip row FOR UPDATE → advisory(trip) → the trip's receipts FOR UPDATE by id
- online collection and van sale: trip row FOR UPDATE → advisory(trip) → insert
- offline receipt: advisory(trip) → settled check → insert
- deposit: its receipts FOR UPDATE by id → settled check
- undo: its one receipt FOR UPDATE → settled check

No path waits for a lock while holding one that an earlier path in this order wants, so no cycle is possible. READ COMMITTED re-reads a locked row once the lock is granted, so the loser always sees the winner's committed status or trip state.

## Binding amendments

- (a) The receipt row `FOR UPDATE` is the only lock a money move holds on receipts, and the trip's state is read only under it. `depositReceipts`, `undoReceipt` and `tripMoney({ lock: true })` lock in ascending `receipts.id`. No path takes a trip lock after a receipt lock; any future receipt writer follows the same order.
- (b) The advisory lock is `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext('trip-money:<tenantId>:<tripId>'))`, transaction-scoped, taken only by `recordReceipt` for a trip receipt and by `tripMoney({ lock: true })`. A hash collision can only over-serialise, never mis-lock.
- (c) `depositReceipts` refuses the WHOLE batch when any row moved (`receipt_moved`, 409); nothing is partially banked. The loser of a plain race sees the existing "is deposited, not collected" sentence.
- (d) Settlement counts `receipts` carrying the trip's id, net of reversals, per mode. `collections` is stop-level detail and is never summed for money again. T5 and T7 pin Cr CASH_VAN = Σ Dr CASH_VAN.
- (e) An undo's source follows the ONE DEFINITION: BANK if deposited; CASH if it is trip cash and the trip is in either settled state; otherwise the landing account. It is decided under the row lock.
- (f) A receipt for a trip that has settled is refused after the replay check: 409 `trip_settled` online, a `trip_settled` sync rejection offline, so a re-sent op that already landed is still a replay. This is pending FOUNDER QUESTION 1; under answer B or C, T4 and T11 swap expectations.
