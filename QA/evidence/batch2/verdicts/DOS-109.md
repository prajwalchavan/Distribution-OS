## Amendments (binding)
(a) **Write the wire word, not the column word, into the plan-change payload.** subscriptions.service.ts:193 becomes `status: statusToWire(before.status)` (helper already in internals.ts:135), so a new `subscription.updated` row's `from.status` is `trialing`/`active`/… exactly like its top-level `status`. `word.trial` stays in strings.ts for rows already on disk, and `audit.test.ts` keeps one fixture with `from.status: 'trial'` (dos_qa copy) and adds one with `'trialing'`; both read "On trial". This is the second cause: the trail mixed two vocabularies and the plan was about to bake the schema enum into the app.
(b) **A `support.withdrawn` line never says the owner refused.** In `auditChange`, the right-hand side is always `word('revoked')` ("Handed back"): every `support.withdrawn` row is the console handing a window back, and `now: 'rejected'` only means the owner had not yet answered (`statusOf`, support-grants.ts:20). The left side stays `word(was)`; `now` remains visible in the "Everything recorded" panel. The shared `word.rejected` is untouched (DOS-110).
(c) **`actorName` is never a guess.** `platformAdminNames` returns only what the 0037 function returns; the mapper writes `names.get(id) ?? null` and the app falls back to `word(actorRole)`. No client-side directory, no `admin.users.list` call from the audit screen.
(d) **Typed vocabulary is complete or the build fails.** `PLATFORM_AUDIT_ACTIONS` lists the nine actions; `writePlatformAudit`, `insertPlatformAuditUnderSystem`, `setStatus`'s `audit.action`, the raw `disableUser` insert and support-audit.interceptor.ts:74 all name `PlatformAuditAction`. The core spec's case 1 asserts every returned production action is in the array; DOS-107 must add `user.enabled` and its word key when it lands.
(e) **The raw inserts keep `gen_random_uuid()` in this fix.** Do not change id minting or the cursor here (new finding 1 below); the plan's edit to those two statements is the typed action parameter only.
