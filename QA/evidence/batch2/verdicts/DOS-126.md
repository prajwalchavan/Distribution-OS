# DOS-126 — architect verdict (Fable, 2026-09-13)
**Verdict:** APPROVE WITH AMENDMENTS

Refutation attempted and failed. Root cause holds at every citation: `priceOrderLines` (orders/pricing-lines.ts:139-217) is the only writer of
rate/discount/applied_rules/price_locked, called only by `writeLines` (orders.service.ts:597-628) on drafts; `confirmInTx` (:337-391) reserves the
stored lines and updates the header without totals; `approvals.decide` (:102-128) → `decideInTx` (bargains.service.ts:185-196) sets status and
approved_rate only; `QuoteService.quote()` opens its own `withTenant` (quote.service.ts:67-71), so a rate approved in the deciding transaction is
invisible to it (probe S2/S5); the engine matches by variant and a final OVERRIDE does not block it (schemes.ts:301-317; `resolveBase` sets only
`schemesAllowed`). The second defect is real and is NOT confined to the C1 path: every rep ask auto-approved inside its bound
(bargains.service.ts:111-141) already stores rate = bargain rate (:192) AND discount including the bargain (:174), and `priceOrderLineGroup` bills
rate × qty − discount (invoices.service.ts:1419-1420), so those bills are under-charged today; `line_total − tax` (:175, :197) is the only invariant
both stored conventions satisfy. `asSystem` (orders.internals.ts:192-206) sets only the DB setting, never the ALS context, so the plan's claim that
`quoteInTx` keeps the shop's ctx (loadRetailer link check) while the line update runs as DB role system (staffWritePolicy, columns.ts:227-240) is
correct. dos_qa, read-only: 0 API-convention bargained lines, 27 seed-convention, 26 invoice lines with a bargain rule, all seed-shaped.
Engine reprice over a stored delta is the right call: the bargain step is additive and last (on gross, independent of scheme money), so on an
unchanged catalog the engine result equals drafted + bargain exactly, and a list-rate edit in between moves only the list/discount split, never the
net (net = approved rate × qty − scheme). Only a scheme edit can move the changed line, and the plan's "net must fall" rule bounds that.

## Decisions
- technical: reprice through the engine on the caller's tx (`quoteInTx`), on the IST date of the lines' created_at, honouring pricingDateMode —
  one engine (CLAUDE.md), no re-implementation of the final-scheme/base-rate rules from stored applied_rules.
- technical: no contract, permission or schema change; `quoteInTx` and `approvedBargainsFor` are exported through pricing/index.ts like
  `pendingBargainsForOrder`; orders never reads bargain_requests itself. Lines keep their ids (reservations, picks, device rows follow); the 0040
  touch trigger (clock_timestamp) makes the sales and retailer devices pull the new rate with no tombstone.
- technical: the billing correction ships in this plan, not as a separate finding — it is live today for auto-approved asks, and the confirm half
  alone would make every approved rate an under-declared bill. Existing rows and issued invoices are not repaired (Q5 rebuild; immutability).
- FOUNDER DEFAULT: at confirm the server applies the drafted prices plus any rate approved since the draft, and nothing else. An office edit to a
  price list or scheme between draft and approval does not re-price a held order, and a line changes only when its net goes down, so a credit
  decision taken on the drafted total is never exceeded. This is how docs/22 §4 "Server re-prices at the same version" is read; record it in §8
  beside the DOS-126 row. Revisit only if DOS-082 decides to re-price catalog edits at submit.
- FOUNDER DEFAULT: a shop's own standalone approved rate (the retailer app asks with no order id, order.tsx:272-281) is charged on that shop's
  held order at confirm, exactly as the engine already applies it at draft.
