## Decisions
- technical: 1(A). Additive read `orders.lastPlaced`, GET `/orders/last-placed`, input `{ retailerId }`, output `{ item: OrderDetail | null }`,
  `ANY_MEMBER` placed right after `'orders.list'` (permissions.ts:525). `repeatLast` keeps its shape and composes the same
  `lastPlacedOrder(tx, retailerId, salespersonId?)` helper in orders.internals.ts (module-internal, after `listOrders`).
- technical: 2(a). "Last" = `coalesce(submitted_at, created_at) DESC, id DESC` over `state NOT IN ('draft','cancelled')`, tenant predicate
  explicit — the exact sort Home already uses (index.tsx:95). Not `created_at`: a draft saved on Monday and sent on Friday was placed Friday.
- technical: 4(a). The DOS-073 reach rule applies to `lastPlaced` (a rep gets its own newest placed order for the shop, else `null`);
  `repeatLast` stays author-agnostic in this slice — its reach gap is filed below, not folded in.
- technical: pieces are preserved on the device through R7's own case/piece conversion with the CURRENT case size; `enteredQty/enteredUnit`
  of the old line are never forwarded. Per-tap nonce in the URL, key `['orders','last-placed', retailerId, nonce]`, `staleTime: 0`.
- technical: no migration, no index, no schema change; 0045 stays free.
- FOUNDER DEFAULT: 3(a). Any author and any placed source counts (rep, shop, phone, WhatsApp, van sale): docs/22 §4 R1 → S5 "reorder" and
  docs/06 "Reorder last order" both mean the shop's last order, and Home's "Your last orders" already lists rep orders.
