## Decisions
- technical: predicate registration over a receipts column or an outbox consumer — no migration, no backfill, same-transaction truth, fail closed.
- technical: `withCrew` is `boolean | null` on get (null outside MONEY_DESK) and tri-state on list, desk-only per (b). ReceiptSchema unchanged.
- technical: settled-trip cash banks Dr BANK / Cr CASH (settlement already moved it CASH_VAN → CASH). Receipt posting (:660) and reversal
  (:1744-1746) stay as they are in this fix; the reversal half is filed below.
- technical: `cancelled` is not "handed over"; a receipt naming a cancelled or unknown trip is never bankable (repair = reverse and re-record).
- FOUNDER DEFAULT: a cheque taken on a trip, like its cash, is banked (and bounced from Day-end) only after the trip settles; the receipt panel's
  own Mark bounced stays available. Chosen because the crew physically holds the cheque until check-in and docs/22 §6:189 says trip money reaches
  the bank only after settlement. Record in docs/22 §8 with the batch-2 defaults; reversible by limiting the deposit check to `mode = 'cash'`.
