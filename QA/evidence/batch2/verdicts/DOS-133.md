# DOS-133 — architect verdict (Fable, 2026-09-13)
**Verdict:** APPROVE WITH AMENDMENTS

The design holds. Root cause confirmed on main: `PackingService.list` (packing.service.ts:176,182) pages and orders on the device-made id, seeded packs carry hash ids (dos_qa SO-0862 = `a4e4ef5b…`, live SO-0909 = `01a0979b…`), W7 (load/index.tsx:56) reads an unfiltered page of 50 and never pages. The `awaiting_load` rule = `loadSheets.create`'s own acceptance (load-sheets.service.ts:165-189 + `onALiveSheet` :754-767) is right in both directions: `dispatchIfPacked` (trips.service.ts:844-858) dispatches with no sheet, and `return_undelivered` leaves a `packed` order on a confirmed sheet (dos_qa today: SO-0904, SO-0899, SO-0857, SO-0850, SO-0845 are `packed` with a live sheet; the oracle gives SO-0909, SO-0883, SO-0892, SO-0862). No second cause: W7 is online-only through `@dos/api-client`; no sync handler, worker job or RLS policy is on the path, and STOCK_VIEWERS (permissions.ts:89-95, :617) already read all three tables. Contract change is additive; no permission or RLS change; one index migration. The cross-module SQL predicate (`OrdersService.orderInState`) is approved as the ONE precedent — the fragment is built inside orders/fulfilment.ts, warehouse only passes its own column, and the bounded-id-list fallback would be silently wrong once returned bills pile up.
