## Decisions
- technical (1a): one additive read `delivery.trips.planning`, GET `/delivery/trip-planning` (never under `/delivery/trips/`), crew + bills, cursor ≤ 200.
- technical (2a): `ROLE_GROUPS.STOCK_KEEPERS` (owner, manager, warehouse); core `TRIP_BOARD` adds `system`. The crew plans only its van-only day and
  never reads the board; the accountant reads the register (STOCK_VIEWERS) and nothing else on the tab.
- technical (3): yes — the role-proof guard belongs to DOS-131 (the W10 path opens the hole); one shared ids-only helper for guard and board, plus (b).
- technical (4): (a) as amended by (c). No server check: warehouse cannot read delivery, and a delivery-side wrapper is not worth a second procedure.
- technical (5): NO. The board lists `packed` only; with (c) the app no longer creates dispatched-but-unplanned orders; the one in dos_qa is fixture state.
- FOUNDER DEFAULT: from the warehouse app a load sheet is always for one planned/loading trip and carries only that trip's bills; a trip-less sheet
  stays API-only (seed, smoke). Consistent with docs/22 2026-09-12 (godown plans, adds stops, starts loading) and W6 "load sheet for the trip".
