## The shape of the gate from here

An absolute count over the whole tenant's data state is the wrong shape for a LANE gate: it fails for reasons the lane cannot reach and passes for reasons the lane did not earn. From this ruling, a lane's smoke gate has three parts, and the walk stage states them per lane: (a) the design's NAMED operations read OK (never EXPECTED, never a 409 counted as green) on a fresh seed and on a replay; (b) **no NEW BROKEN against main at the merge base** — same seed, same `--run-tag`, first run — with every BROKEN that also appears on main filed as an S-row in the same turn, never swallowed; (c) nothing turns BROKEN on the replay. The absolute "0 BROKEN" survives as a MAIN-health gate the integrator runs once after each merge of main, and it becomes binding only after the observed line above exists. P3 for the harness: `--baseline <dir>` that diffs against a stored run so (b) is a printed list rather than an agent's eyes.
