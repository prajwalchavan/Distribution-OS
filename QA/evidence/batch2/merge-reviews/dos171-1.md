## Blockers

1. **The new cash-discount line names an amount the bill never takes off.** Location: `frontend/delivery-app/app/stop/[id]/van-sale.tsx:322-333`, plus `src/strings.ts:290`.
   - Where the line's figure comes from: `quote.cashDiscountPaise`, the best `cash_discount_pct` SCHEME applied to the NET of the lines it covers (domain `pricing/schemes.ts:345-367`).
   - What the bill actually uses: `retailers.cash_discount_bps` applied to the bill TOTAL (`billing.internals.ts:280` → `invoices.service.ts:1563`, then `allocation.ts:249-250`, `percentOf(total, bps)`).
   - No scheme cash discount ever reaches a bill. The only `cash_discount_conditions` insert is `receivables.service.ts:538`.
   - Result, case 1: a shop with a scheme but no profile rate is told "Paid now, ₹X comes off the bill", pays X less, and the bill stays `partially_paid`. That is the DOS-171 short collection again.
   - Result, case 2: when both exist at 2 % on S-28, the line says ₹4.43 but allocation takes ₹4.96.
   - Result, case 3: a profile-rate shop gets no line at all. The seed has all three cases (`seed-demo/retailers.ts:343-367` at 200/250 bps; `seed-demo/pricing.ts:362,572` schemes).
   - **Fix:**
     - Delete the `d6-cash-discount` `Txt` block (:322-333), the now-unused `formatINR` (:41) and `paise` (:46) imports, and the `d6.cashDiscount` string.
     - Either keep `SaleFigures.cashDiscountPaise`, or remove it together with its `toEqual` key (van-sale.test.ts:99).
     - Add `expect.soft(code, 'D6 names a cash discount').not.toMatch(/\bcashDiscount/)` to the source guard.
   - This amends design 2b. (c) still holds: cash discount is never deducted from Bill total. The docs/22 row does not mention cash discount. What D5 shows a cash-discount shop stays S-item (1).
2. **Gate after fix 1, on the merged tree.** Neither the app builder nor its verifier ran these.
   - Frontend: `pnpm lint && pnpm typecheck && pnpm test && pnpm build && pnpm format:check`, plus a native (Hermes) export of `@dos/delivery-app`.
   - Backend: `pnpm --filter @dos/core build && pnpm --filter @dos/core test -- src/modules/delivery`, then lint, typecheck and format:check.

## Defects outside the lane

1. **P1** `frontend/delivery-app/app/stop/[id]/van-sale.tsx:263` (+ :184-185, :190). `create.mutate({ at: Date.now() })` makes every tap a new intent, and the run mints fresh invoice, delivery and line ids. A retry after a lost reply (20 s deadline) therefore issues a SECOND bill and takes the van stock twice. Fix: a stable input (the draft lines) and ids derived from `meta.id`.
2. **P2** `frontend/retailer-app/app/order.tsx:560-573`. R7 prints the quote's scheme cash discount ("Pay within {days} days and {amount} comes off"), but the bill applies `retailers.cash_discount_bps` to the total. A `cash_discount_pct` scheme is reported on quotes and orders and never realised on a bill (schemes.ts:345-367 against receivables.service.ts:538).
3. **P2** `frontend/delivery-app/app/stop/[id]/collect.tsx:100`. D5's headline never subtracts receipts already taken at this door. After deliver, collect and then a top-up van sale, it asks for the paid bill again plus the new one. Pre-existing; for lean-delivery-collect (14).
4. **P3** `frontend/delivery-app/app/index.tsx:109-111`. 'Still to collect' drops a door as soon as it is delivered or van-billed, before any money is taken. A product question for lean-delivery-collect (14).
