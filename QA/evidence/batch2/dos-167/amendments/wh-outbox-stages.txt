== A DEFECT FOUND WHILE OPERATING THE APP (not part of the keep claim) ==
The picking sheet LOCKS OUT PICKING whenever it is opened while the office is unreachable, even though the
wave and all 735 of its lines are already on the phone. The rows render, the promise
"Picks are saved on this phone and sent when there is a signal." is printed, and the app adds
"Still filling this phone from the server" — and the Picked / Short buttons are simply absent.
Measured: locked from 16:56:20 to 17:02:08 (5 m 48 s, polled every 10 s, screenshot wh-32), and it unlocked
within seconds of the office becoming reachable again ("Updated just now", screenshot wh-33, same row, same
wave, buttons back). A pick recorded BEFORE the cut, on a sheet already open, works fine (that is how both
kept ops above were made) — so the lock is on OPENING the sheet without a signal, not on picking offline.
Business impact: a picker whose phone restarts, or who walks into the sheet in a dead corner of the godown,
cannot record a single piece until the signal returns.
