## RETRACTED — "Take order does nothing on Android" was NOT a product bug. It was LogBox.

Written mid-walk, then disproved in the same walk. Keeping it here because the first half is what the
screenshots show and someone reading them would otherwise draw the wrong conclusion.

WHAT WAS SEEN (true)
  tip 8092048, sales app, Pixel_7_API_36, Amit Pawar, shop R-0031 Prerna Super Market, office cut.
  "Take order" (testID take-order) and later "Save on this phone" (testID place-order) did nothing:
  11 taps by resource-id and one W3C touch (pointerDown / 120 ms / pointerUp, origin = the element)
  left the screen where it was, with no JS error in Metro's log.
  The screen was NOT frozen: tapping the in-screen "Orders 25" tab swapped the panel to the order list
    BEFORE tail: ... How old the dues are | as of 12 Sep | Money owed, by age ...
    AFTER  tail: ... SO-0854, 11 Sep · Delivered ... SO-0817, 7 Sep · Delivered ...
  The route was fine too — the same screen opened at once through the app's own link:
    adb shell am start -a android.intent.action.VIEW -d 'dos-sales://orders/new?retailerId=8ddf5b3f-...'
  Screens: B4-stuck.png, probe3-beat.png, probe7-deeplink.png.

WHAT IT ACTUALLY WAS
  React Native's LogBox collapsed banner ("Open debugger to view warnings.") — a DEV-BUILD artifact of
  running the debug APK off Metro. UiAutomator reports its bounds as [26,2146][1054,2271], but it
  swallows presses on the app's whole bottom bar region, which is where BOTH of those buttons live.
  The task brief warned about exactly this: "LogBox draws over the footer — dismiss it rather than
  tapping through."
  Dismiss the banner first and both buttons work on the first tap:
    F1-take-order-retest.txt  — logbox dismissed -> "Take order" -> "Prerna Super Market | Order |
    Items: 0 | Repeat last order (6) | This order | Nothing on this order yet | Add items ..."
    F1-take-order-after-logbox-dismissed.png
    /tmp save probe, 21:57:35Z — logbox dismissed -> "Save on this phone" -> "Offline since 3:12 am ·
    2 waiting to send | Saved on this phone" (B6-queued.png)

NOTHING TO FILE. A release build has no LogBox.
The walk's own steps therefore dismiss the banner before touching any footer button, and the two order
entries in this evidence were reached one by deep link (before the cause was known) and one by the
"Take order" button (after).
