## What could NOT be driven on this emulator
The proof PHOTOGRAPH. `d4-photo` opens the system camera (expo-image-picker -> com.android.camera2).
Twice — 02:27:01 and 02:39:15 — Android killed the delivery app while the camera was foregrounded
(`ActivityManager: Process in.distributionos.delivery ... has died: fg BTOP`, d181-36), so the app
relaunched on Home and the captured proof was gone. That is an emulator memory limit (3 GB, dev
bundle + camera), not a product fault, and it is why the online press was made at the cash shop and
the offline press at the credit shop with "Nothing from this bill" (a failed delivery needs no
photograph by the screen's own rule: `podRequired = ... && outcome !== 'failed'`).
